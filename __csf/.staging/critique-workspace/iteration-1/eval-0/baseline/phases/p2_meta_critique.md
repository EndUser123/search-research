# Phase 2: Meta-Critique of the Critique

Critique the Phase 1 critique — not the original work. Focus on the quality of analysis, not finding new issues in the work itself.

Review the Phase 1 critique and identify:
1. Where Phase 1 was too generic, vague, or lacked specific evidence
2. Where Phase 1 missed adversarial angles or was too lenient
3. Points in Phase 1 that are weakly justified or likely incorrect
4. What the critique failed to question or challenge

Output your meta-critique in this exact structure:

## Input

Read the inputs via file paths:

**Original Work:** `cat "{WORK_FILE}"`
**Phase 1 Critique:** `cat "{PHASE1_FILE}"`

## Output Schema

Return your meta-critique in this exact structure:

```
## Critique Quality Issues
1. [weakness in the critique's reasoning or specificity]
...

## Missed Angles
1. [adversarial angles or perspectives the critique failed to explore]
...

## Improvements to the Critique
1. [specific improvement suggestion 1]
2. [specific improvement suggestion 2]
...
```
