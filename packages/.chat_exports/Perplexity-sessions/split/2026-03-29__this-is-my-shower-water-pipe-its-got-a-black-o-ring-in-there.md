## Human

This is my shower water pipe. It's got a black O-ring in there that seems to be disintegrating. Can I remove that, or do I need it?

## Assistant

You do need an O‑ring (or similar washer) there, but you can and should remove the old, deteriorated one and replace it.

### What that O‑ring does
- It seals the connection between the shower arm (that pipe in the wall) and whatever screws onto it (usually the shower head or a drop‑ear fitting inside the wall).  
- Without a good seal, you’ll get leaks at the joint and water can run back into the wall cavity.

### What you should do
1. Unscrew and pull out the old black O‑ring and any loose bits of rubber.
2. Clean the inside lip and threads with a cloth; don’t scratch the sealing surface.
3. Take the old ring (or a close measurement) to a hardware store and match:
   - Inner diameter (must fit snugly over the male part / into the recess)  
   - Thickness (similar cross‑section so it compresses properly)  
   - Material: EPDM or nitrile are typical for hot water.
4. Install the new O‑ring in the same groove, then reinstall your shower head or connector.
5. Hand‑tighten, then snug gently with a wrench if needed; test for leaks with the water on.
