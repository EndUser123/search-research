## Human

OpenRouter/DALL-E 3, is this free on openrouter?

## Assistant

DALL·E 3 on OpenRouter is not generally “free” the way some text models are; it’s treated as a paid OpenAI image model, so you should expect per‑image charges funded from your OpenRouter credits rather than a zero‑cost tier. OpenRouter does have a set of free models, but these are almost entirely text (and some vision) LLMs from providers like Meta, Google, Qwen, DeepSeek, etc., and current public free‑model lists do not include DALL·E 3 among the zero‑cost options.[1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Current model pricing picture

- OpenRouter exposes GPT Image / DALL·E‑style models under its image section, and cost calculators treat GPT Image 1.x and DALL·E 3 as metered, per‑image models rather than free ones.[3]
- OpenAI’s own published DALL·E 3 pricing (when used directly via OpenAI) falls in roughly the \$0.04–\$0.12 per image range depending on size and quality, which aligns with third‑party cost guides that assume similar non‑zero pricing when proxied through aggregators like OpenRouter.[6]

## OpenRouter free tier vs DALL·E 3

- OpenRouter’s “free models” program offers a curated list (currently a few dozen) of text and some multimodal LLMs at \$0.00 cost, with rate limits such as ~20 requests/minute and ~200 requests/day, and explicitly notes that only *select* models qualify as free.[5][1]
- Public free‑model listings for 2026 enumerate many LLMs (Nemotron, Qwen, Llama 3.x, DeepSeek, Mistral, etc.) but do not list DALL·E 3 or other OpenAI image models among the free entries, indicating DALL·E 3 is not part of the zero‑cost set on OpenRouter.[2][4][1]

## Practical check / next step

- To be certain for your account and region (and in case OpenRouter adds promos later), the definitive check is: open the OpenRouter model catalog, search for “DALL‑E 3” or the relevant OpenAI image model name, and inspect the “Image Cost” column; if it shows non‑zero pricing, it is not free, even if some other models in your account are marked “free.”[4][7]
