## Assistant

Groq offers a free tier for its API with rate limits that vary by model and are applied at the organization level.

## Free Tier Limits
These limits ensure fair access and service stability, but exact values depend on the specific model (e.g., Llama 3.1 variants) and can change. Users must check their console at console.groq.com/settings/limits for personalized, current details. Older reports mention examples like 30 requests per minute (RPM), 14,400 requests per day (RPD), and 40,000 tokens per minute (TPM) for some setups, or around 131k tokens per minute/day for certain large models like Llama 3.1 405B in free previews.[1][2][3][5]

## Checking Your Limits
Log into the Groq console to view real-time limits per model, as they apply per organization and update dynamically. Free tier supports generous inference but upgrades to Developer or Enterprise tiers unlock higher consumption. Reddit discussions confirm variability, with some free users hitting token caps quickly on popular models.[2][3]
