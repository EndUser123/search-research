## Human

What are the most popular extensions to get transcripts from youtube videos that don't have transcripts.

## Assistant

Several popular Chrome extensions generate AI-powered transcripts for YouTube videos lacking built-in captions, ideal for your video content analysis workflows.[1][2]

## Top Extensions
TubeOnAI leads for accuracy across 40+ languages, generating transcripts directly on videos without existing captions and supporting exports like TXT or PDF.[1]
Glasp and YouTube Transcript (ChatGPT-powered) enable one-click extraction and summaries, working on any video regardless of native transcripts.[3][4][1]
Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai add collaboration features like searchable highlights, suitable for batch processing in your notebook audits.[5][1]

## Comparison Table

| Extension     | Accuracy      | Key Features                  | Export Options     | Browser Support |

| TubeOnAI     | Very High    | 40+ languages, summaries     | TXT, PDF, DOCX    | Chrome, Firefox [1] |

## Installation Tips
Install from the Chrome Web Store by searching the extension name; most activate via a sidebar button on YouTube pages.[7][3]
For your VS Code and automation setup, pair with yt-fts or YouTubeTranscriptApi for scripted extraction beyond browser use.[8]
