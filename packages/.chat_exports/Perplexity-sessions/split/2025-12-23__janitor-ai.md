Janitor AI is a web-based chatbot/roleplay platform where users create and chat with customizable AI characters, often used for long-form, persona-driven conversations including NSFW text.[1][2]

## What Janitor AI Is  
- Janitor AI is a **chatbot** platform launched in 2023 that lets users build and interact with AI characters tailored to specific personas and scenarios.[2][1]
- It focuses on natural-language chat, using LLMs under the hood to simulate distinct personalities, backstories, and dialogue styles for each character.[1][2]

## How It Works  
- Users select or create characters, define personality, lore, and example dialogues, then connect them to one of several LLM backends (via built‑in options or external proxies).[3][2][1]
- The system uses NLP and ML to maintain context and generate responses that stay aligned with the specified persona and scenario over long chats.[2][3]

## Content And NSFW Aspects
