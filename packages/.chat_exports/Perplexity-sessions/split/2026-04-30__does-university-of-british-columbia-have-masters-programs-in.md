## Human

Does University of British Columbia have master's programs in systems thinking or cognitive models or in architecture, software architecture, or program management?

## Assistant

Here's what I found for master's programs at the University of British Columbia. They have a graduate program in cognitive science that focuses on perception, memory, thought, language, and action. For software architecture, they offer a Software Systems master's program, as well as a Master of Engineering Leadership in Dependable Software Systems, both of which include training in software architecture. They don't seem to have a specific master's program labeled “systems thinking” or “program management,” but they do have a Master of Management that focuses on strategic thinking, leadership, and decision-making skills, which could be relevant for program management.
