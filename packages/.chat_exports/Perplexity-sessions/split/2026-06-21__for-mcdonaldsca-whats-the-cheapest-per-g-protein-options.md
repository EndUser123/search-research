## Key Takeaways

- **McDouble ($3.89, 21g)** is the single best protein value on the menu at **~$0.185/g** — two beef patties and a slice of cheese in a compact format.
- **Big Mac ($4.49, 24g)** is nearly tied at **$0.187/g**, delivering more total protein per order.
- **Double Hamburger ($3.59, 18g)** is the cheapest dollar-amount option in the top tier at **$0.199/g**.
- **Chicken items are generally poor value** for protein — McNuggets in particular are expensive per gram (battered coating adds carbs/fat without protein).
- **Double Quarter Pounder with Cheese** ($11.39, 47g) is the best option if you want a large protein hit in one item, at a comparable per-gram rate to the Cheeseburger.
