## Human

when I move the mouse to the edge of the screen, the side tab slides out and is annoying.

## Assistant

That sliding behavior is VertiTab’s “auto-show on hover” style feature: when your mouse hits the screen edge, the collapsed sidebar opens.[1][2]

## What to change

On the VertiTab Options page you have open, look for a setting like:
- “Auto-show sidebar,” “Show sidebar on hover,” “Expand on hover,” or similar under sections such as “Sidebar,” “Appearance,” or “Behavior.”[2][1]
- Change that option to either:
  - “Open/close with shortcut only” (then use the keyboard shortcut, often something like Ctrl+Shift+S or a configurable hotkey), or
  - “Always show sidebar” so it stays pinned open instead of sliding in and out on hover.[1][2]

## If you cannot find it

If none of the toggles mention hover or auto-show:
- Set VertiTab to “sidebar always visible” (or similar wording) and then manually resize the sidebar thinner, so it doesn’t cover much content but also doesn’t animate in and out.[2][1]
- Optionally assign and use the “toggle sidebar” keyboard shortcut instead of relying on auto-show at the edge.[1][2]

If you tell what headings you see on the Options page (e.g., “General / Sidebar / Advanced”), guidance can point you exactly where to click.
