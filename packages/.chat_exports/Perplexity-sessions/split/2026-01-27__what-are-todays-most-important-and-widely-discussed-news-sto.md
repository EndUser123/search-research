**Today's Top News Stories (January 27, 2026)**

The most discussed stories center on a fatal police shooting in Minnesota sparking national outrage, a deadly winter storm disrupting the U.S. South, North Korea's missile test, and the recovery of the last Israeli hostage's remains from Gaza.[1][2][3]

### Minnesota ICE Shooting
Border Patrol commander Greg Bovino was removed from Minnesota after agents fatally shot Alex Pretti, a 37-year-old ICU nurse with a legal firearm permit, during immigration enforcement protests. Video evidence shows Pretti filming agents pushing protesters and attempting to help a woman sprayed with mace before being shot, contradicting DHS claims he aimed to "massacre law enforcement.". The incident, the second U.S. citizen killing by federal forces there this month, has fueled bipartisan protests and a White House reassessment, with border czar Tom Homan now overseeing operations.[4][5][6][7][8]

### Deadly Winter Storm
A massive ice and snow storm has killed at least 34-38 people across multiple states, including drownings in frozen ponds and tree collapses, with over 540,000 still without power in the South. Areas like Mississippi, Tennessee, Louisiana, and Texas face prolonged outages as ice weighs down lines—half an inch adding up to 500 pounds—with restoration expected to take days amid hazardous conditions. National Guard deployments continue as frigid temperatures persist, exacerbating the crisis.[2][9][10][11][12]

### North Korea Missile Test
North Korea launched two short-range ballistic missiles into the Sea of Japan early Tuesday, traveling about 350 km before splashing down outside Japan's economic zone. The test, the second this month, follows a hypersonic missile claim and coincides with U.S. defense visits to allies, prompting protests from Seoul and Tokyo.[13][14][15][16]

### Gaza Hostage Recovery
Israel recovered the remains of Ran Gvili, the last hostage held in Gaza, from a northern cemetery, enabling the ceasefire's next phase after Hamas provided location details. Gvili, a 24-year-old police officer killed on October 7, 2023, completes the return of all captives, living or dead, in exchange for Palestinian bodies.[17][18]
