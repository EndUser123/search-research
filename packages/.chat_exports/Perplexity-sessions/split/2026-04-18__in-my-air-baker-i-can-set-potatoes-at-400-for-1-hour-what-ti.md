## Human

In my air baker I can set potatoes at 400 for 1 hour.  What time should I use for 325?

## Assistant

Based on cooking temperature adjustments for air fryers, if you're cooking at 325°F instead of 400°F, you should increase the cooking time by approximately **40-50%**.[1][2]

For your specific situation: at 400°F for 1 hour, try **approximately 1 hour 25-30 minutes at 325°F**. 

The exact adjustment depends on your potato size—larger potatoes need more time at lower temperatures. The key indicator is doneness: your potatoes are ready when a fork or knife easily pierces the center or reaches an internal temperature of 200-205°F.[3]

Since lower temperatures cook more gently, checking for tenderness around the 1 hour 15 minute mark is a safe approach—you can add 5-10 minute increments if needed.
