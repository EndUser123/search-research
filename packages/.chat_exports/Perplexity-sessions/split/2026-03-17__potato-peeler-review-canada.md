For Canada right now, the standout choices are the OXO Good Grips swivel‑style peelers (Pro Swivel and standard Swivel) and the Kuhn Rikon Original Swiss Y‑peeler, with OXO slightly ahead for comfort and versatility.[1][2][3][4][5][6][7]

## Top picks that are easy to buy in Canada

- **OXO Good Grips Pro Swivel Peeler (straight)** – Frequently rated “best overall” for potatoes and mixed veg; very sharp, peels thinly, works for left/right hand, has a good potato eye remover, and is built sturdier than the classic OXO. The silicone handle is comfortable, non‑slip when wet, and the swiveling blade glides on potatoes and apples without digging in.[2][3][4]
- OXO Good Grips Swivel Peeler (classic, cheaper) – Similar performance with a slightly simpler build; twin Japanese stainless blades, very comfortable non‑slip handle, dishwasher safe, and widely stocked in Canadian retailers like Crate & Barrel Canada and others.[4][8][7][1]
- Kuhn Rikon Original Swiss Peeler (Y‑style) – Extremely sharp carbon steel blade that excels on potatoes, carrots, and even butternut squash; produces thin peels and stays sharp for a long time, but the exposed blade and small plastic handle feel cheaper and less secure to some users.[3][9][5][6]

### Shape and ergonomics (Y vs straight)

- Straight swivel peelers (OXO Pro/Swivel, Spring Chef, etc.) feel familiar to most home cooks and are great for long strokes along potatoes, carrots, and apples. They tend to be better for detail work and for people used to “knife‑like” motions.[10][2][3]
- Y‑shaped peelers (Kuhn Rikon, OXO Y) are excellent for round items like potatoes, beets, and large vegetables; they can be faster once you get used to the motion, but handles are more hit‑or‑miss for comfort.[11][9][5][10]

## What I’d recommend in Canada

- If you want one **do‑everything** peeler and care about comfort: get the OXO Good Grips Pro Swivel; it’s the most consistently top‑rated and is easy to find from Canadian sellers.[7][2][3]
- If you want something cheaper but still excellent: the standard OXO Good Grips Swivel Peeler gives you 80–90% of the experience for less, and is widely stocked at Canadian big‑box and online retailers.[1][4][7]
- If you prioritize raw cutting performance and don’t mind a bare‑bones handle: add a Kuhn Rikon Original Swiss Peeler as a second tool; it’s fantastic on potatoes and hard squash but less forgiving ergonomically.[9][5][6][3]

Are you optimizing for sheer speed on big batches of potatoes, or mostly casual home cooking where comfort and safety matter more?
