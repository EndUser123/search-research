## Human

for factory droid.ai are there feature differences between free and paid subcriptions?

## Assistant

Factory Droid (Factory.ai) has a free BYOK tier and several paid tiers; the main differences are who pays for compute, how many managed tokens you get, collaboration limits, and level of support.[1][2][3]

## Free (BYOK) plan

- Price: $0/month; you bring your own API keys and pay model providers directly.[2][3][1]
- Core features included: frontier multi‑model “Droid” agent, infinite/large context engine, terminal UI, adjustable autonomy, native IDE integration, headless mode, subagents, slash commands, hooks, and context tagging.[4][1][2]
- No Factory-managed token pool; usage is limited only by your own OpenAI/Anthropic/etc. quotas.[1][2]

## Pro / Max / Ultra paid plans

- Price: Pro $20/mo, Max $200/mo, Ultra $2,000/mo (plus an Enterprise tier).[3][1]
- Managed compute:  
  - Pro: 10M “Factory Standard” tokens/month + 10M bonus.[3][1]
  - Max: 100M + 100M bonus tokens.[1][3]
  - Ultra: 1B + 1B bonus tokens.[3][1]
- Model access: dedicated Factory compute with frontier models and fast priority routing when new models drop, instead of relying only on your own keys.[5][2][1]
- Apps & agents: desktop / web / mobile app, cloud and local background agents, and expanded first‑party integrations.[1]
- Team features: Pro supports up to 50 members (extra seats billed per user), Max up to 100 seats, Ultra and Enterprise scale higher with custom limits; you also get analytics, billing/usage dashboards, and an agent‑readiness dashboard.[3][1]
- Higher tiers add more capacity, early access features, and for Ultra/Enterprise: dedicated support (Slack, live chat), audit logging, enterprise codebase analysis, and advanced repo permissions.[1][3]

If you are fine paying OpenAI/Anthropic directly and mostly want the agent/runtime, the free BYOK tier is usable; if you want “one bill” managed compute, apps, and team features, you need a paid plan.
