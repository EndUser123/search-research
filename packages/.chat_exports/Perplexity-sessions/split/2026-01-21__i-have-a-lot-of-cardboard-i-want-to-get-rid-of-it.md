## Human

I have a lot of cardboard. I want to get rid of it.

## Assistant

Got it! There are a few great ways to get rid of cardboard. You could check if your local recycling program accepts it—most cities have curbside pickup or drop-off locations. Some businesses also accept large amounts of cardboard for recycling. Another option: if the cardboard is in good shape, you can donate it to people who need moving boxes. And if you have a compost pile, small pieces of cardboard can be composted too.
