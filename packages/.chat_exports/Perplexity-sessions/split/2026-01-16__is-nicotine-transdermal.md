## Human

Is nicotine transdermal

## Assistant

**Yes, nicotine is transdermal.** Nicotine skin patches deliver the substance through the skin into the bloodstream to help with smoking cessation. These patches provide a steady release over 16-24 hours, reducing withdrawal symptoms.[1][2]

## Delivery Method
Transdermal nicotine patches apply to clean, dry, hairless skin on areas like the upper chest or arm. The nicotine diffuses across the skin barrier for systemic absorption. Factors like heat can enhance this uptake.[3][4][5]

## Usage Guidelines
Apply one patch daily, rotating sites to avoid irritation, and remove after the recommended time. Common side effects include skin reactions and sleep disturbances. Treatment typically lasts 8-12 weeks with step-down dosing.[6][1]
