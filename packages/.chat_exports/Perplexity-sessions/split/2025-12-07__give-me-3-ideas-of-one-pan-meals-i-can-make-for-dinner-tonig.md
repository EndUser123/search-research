## One-Pan Chicken Thighs with Potatoes and Brussels Sprouts

Savory chicken thighs cook alongside potatoes, Brussels sprouts, and bacon in a single skillet, allowing the bacon fat to infuse all ingredients with rich flavor. Brown the chicken first, then add chopped vegetables and bake until tender, typically in under an hour. This hearty meal serves 4 and uses basic pantry staples.[2]

## One-Pan Mexican Quinoa Skillet

Combine ground beef or turkey, quinoa, black beans, corn, diced tomatoes, and taco seasoning in one skillet for a quick, flavorful vegetarian-adaptable dish. Sauté the protein, stir in rinsed quinoa and liquids, then simmer covered for 20-25 minutes until fluffy. Top with avocado or cheese for extra appeal.[5][2]

## Sheet Pan Lemon Garlic Chicken and Potatoes

Toss bone-in chicken thighs, potatoes, and vegetables with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and herbs on a sheet pan, then roast at 425°F for 40-50 minutes. The chicken renders fat that crisps the potatoes while keeping everything juicy and aromatic. Ideal for minimal prep on busy nights.[6][11]
