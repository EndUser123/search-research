## Human

Maple Scan

## Assistant

Maple Scan is a mobile app designed to help Canadian shoppers identify products that support local businesses by scanning items with their phone camera—no barcodes required.[1][2]

## Key Features
It analyzes a product's brand, ownership history, and Canadian ties using AI, then suggests local alternatives, tariff details, and nearby stores or deals. The app supports English and French, offers scan history, and recently added iOS-specific enhancements like lock screen widgets and accessibility improvements.[2][3]

## Availability
Download it free on the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android), with over 10,000 downloads reported. It's built with SwiftUI for iOS and Kotlin for Android by developer Sasha Ivanov, a UCalgary alum from Calgary.[3][6][2]

## Reception
Users praise its accuracy and usefulness for "buying Canadian-first," with strong App Store ratings and early chart success (#2 in Canadian shopping apps). Reddit discussions highlight its quick development and potential for real-time scanning upgrades.[4][6][3]
