## Human

I need a 1 to 4 kvm switch in canada for less than $150 cdn.  Are there any limitiations I need to know?

## Assistant

Several 1-to-4 port KVM switches are available in Canada under $150 CAD from retailers like Best Buy Canada, Newegg.ca, Canada Computers, and potentially Amazon.ca.  These typically support HDMI or DisplayPort connections for sharing one monitor, keyboard, and mouse across four computers.[1][2][3][4]

## Recommended Options
Affordable models include the StarTech SV431DHD4KU (4-port HDMI, 4K@30Hz, USB 3.0 hub, audio) at Canada Computers and Best Buy Canada, and generic 4-port HDMI KVMs around $70-$85 with cables and remote.  KM-only switches like the ATEN CS724KM offer boundless mouse switching for under $150.  Check Newegg.ca for deals on 4-in-1-out HDMI models at $70-$140 CAD with free shipping.[5][6][2][7][8][3][4]

## Key Limitations
Budget 1-to-4 KVMs often cap at 4K@30Hz or 1080p@60Hz, lacking support for high-refresh rates like 144Hz.  USB is typically 2.0 or 3.0 (slower peripherals), hotkey switching may glitch with complex wireless keyboards/mice, and audio/USB hubs might not handle high-bandwidth devices reliably.  Ensure HDMI/DP compatibility across your Windows 11 setup and four computers; multi-monitor needs dual-output models.  Power via USB may limit stability without a dedicated adapter.[9][10][11][2][7][1]

## Human

Do I need a keyboard/mouse that only uses one usb port, or can I have a mouse and keyboard that each use one usb port?

## Assistant

Standard KVM switches support connecting a keyboard to one dedicated USB HID port and a mouse to another dedicated USB HID port, so separate devices are fine and do not require a single shared USB port like a combo dongle. Most budget 1-to-4 port models, such as those from StarTech or generic HDMI KVMs under $150 CAD, provide at least two dedicated console USB ports specifically for keyboard and mouse inputs.[1][2][10]

## Port Setup
Dedicated USB HID ports (often labeled with keyboard/mouse icons) handle one device each for optimal hotkey switching and emulation across your four Windows 11 computers. Additional USB 2.0/3.0 hub ports on these KVMs allow peripherals but disable hotkeys if used for keyboard/mouse.[2][3][5]

## Potential Issues
Wireless keyboards/mice with separate receivers work but may need USB 2.0+ bandwidth and can cause switching delays (4-15 seconds); wired devices are more reliable. Gaming or multimedia keyboards/mice might lose features on HID ports—test on USB 2.0/3.0 ports as a workaround. Bluetooth devices bypass KVMs entirely, so stick to wired or 2.4GHz USB receivers.[3][4][1]
