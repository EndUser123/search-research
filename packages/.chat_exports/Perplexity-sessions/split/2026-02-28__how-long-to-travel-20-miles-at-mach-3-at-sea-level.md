## Human

How long to travel 20 miles at Mach 3 at sea level

## Assistant

Traveling 20 miles at Mach 3 speed at sea level takes about 31 seconds. This calculation uses the speed of sound at sea level, approximately 767 mph, making Mach 3 around 2,301 mph.[1][2]

## Speed Breakdown
Mach 3 equals three times the speed of sound, which is roughly 767 mph under standard sea-level conditions (15°C).[3][1]
This yields a ground speed of about 2,301 mph.[2][4]

## Time Calculation
Distance divided by speed: 20 miles / 2,301 mph = 0.0087 hours.  
Converting gives 0.52 minutes, or precisely 31.3 seconds.
