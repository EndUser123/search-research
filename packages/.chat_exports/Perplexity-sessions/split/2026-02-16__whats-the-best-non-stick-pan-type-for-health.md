## Human

what's the best non-stick pan type for health?

## Assistant

Ceramic-coated non-stick pans are the healthiest option among true non-stick types, as they avoid PFAS chemicals like PTFE found in traditional Teflon, which can release fumes when overheated or leach "forever chemicals" into food. Brands like GreenPan (Thermolon coating) and Caraway use sand-derived ceramics free of PFOA, lead, and cadmium, making them safe for everyday cooking without toxic breakdown.[1][2][3][4][5]

## Health Risks of Alternatives
Traditional PTFE/Teflon pans are PFOA-free now but still contain PFAS; overheating above 500°F (260°C) risks polymer fume fever and chemical exposure. Ceramic coatings don't degrade this way and handle higher heat better, though they may lose non-stick over time (1-3 years).[4][5][6][1]

## Top Recommendations
- **GreenPan**: Pioneer in PFAS-free ceramic; even heating, oven-safe, 10-year warranty on some.[3][1]
- **Caraway**: Durable ceramic, induction-compatible, excellent egg release in tests.[2]
- **Our Place Always Pan**: Sand-based Thermakind coating, versatile for one-pan meals.[1]

For ultimate health, consider naturally non-stick cast iron or carbon steel (seasoned), which add iron to food without coatings. Use low-medium heat, wooden/silicone utensils, and hand-wash to extend life.[7][8][1]
