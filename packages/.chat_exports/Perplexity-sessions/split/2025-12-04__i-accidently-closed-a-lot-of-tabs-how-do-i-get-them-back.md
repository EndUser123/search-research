## Human

i accidently closed a lot of tabs.  how do i get them back?

## Assistant

The fastest way to get your tabs back is to use the keyboard shortcut or the browser's history menu. Since you appear to be using **TabManager.io**, I have also included instructions for recovering sessions through that extension.

### Method 1: Keyboard Shortcut (Fastest)
Press this key combination immediately to reopen the last closed tab or window. **Press it multiple times** to keep reopening older tabs one by one.

*   **Windows / Linux:** `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `T`
*   **Mac:** `Cmd` + `Shift` + `T`

### Method 2: Chrome History Menu
If the shortcut doesn't bring back the specific tabs you want, check your "Recently Closed" list:

1.  Click the **three dots** icon (menu) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
2.  Hover over **History**.
3.  Look under the **Recently Closed** section.
4.  You will see individual tabs or groups listed (e.g., "5 tabs"). Click on them to restore the entire group at once.[1][2]

### Method 3: Using TabManager.io
Since you are currently on the **TabManager.io** dashboard (`chrome-extension://...`), you can likely find your lost tabs in a saved session:

1.  Look for a **History** or **Sessions** tab within the TabManager.io interface.
2.  Find a session timestamped **before** you accidentally closed the tabs.
3.  Click the **Restore** or **Open** button next to that session to bring all those tabs back at once.
4.  *Note:* Most tab managers automatically save sessions periodically, so you should have a recent backup available there even if the browser history was cleared.[3]
