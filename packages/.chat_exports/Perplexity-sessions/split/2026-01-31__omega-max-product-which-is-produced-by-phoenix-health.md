There are multiple “Omega Max” supplements on the market, and none appears to be made by a company called “Phoenix Health” specifically; the one that is most plausibly related is **Accentrate Omega Max** from **Fenix Health Science**, which is an omega‑3 product aimed at brain/attention support rather than a generic fish‑oil.[1][2][3][4]

### Likely product you’re seeing

- **Brand/company:** Fenix Health Science, which markets Accentrate‑branded omega‑3 formulas for attention, mood, and cognitive support.[2][3][4]
- **Product name:** Accentrate Omega Max (sometimes shown simply as “Omega Max” in their omega product line).[3][4][1]
- **Positioning:** “Maximum absorption” omega‑3 for people needing higher-dose or better‑absorbed EPA/DHA, targeted at attention, memory, and mood rather than just cardiovascular health.[4][1][3]

### What’s distinctive about this Omega Max

- Uses omega‑3s in **LPC‑EPA and LPC‑DHA** form (LYSOVETA) to enhance delivery to the brain, with marketing focused on “maximum brain absorption.”[4]
- Intended daily dose is typically **3 softgels per day**, with or without food, under medical supervision for some users.[4]
- Framed as higher‑potency / higher‑absorption compared with standard fish oil in the same brand’s line (Omega, Omega 110, Omega Max).[3][4]

### Important clarifications

- **Phoenix vs Fenix:** Phoenix Health (e.g., phoenix.ca, a Canadian men’s telehealth clinic) does not list an in‑house “Omega Max” product under that name; if your packaging says Phoenix Health, it may be a **private‑labeled** version or a different company entirely.[5]
- There are **many** unrelated “Omega Max” or “OmegaMAX” products (standard high‑EPA/DHA fish oils, mixed omega‑3/6/9 formulas, etc.), so you must match by **label** (logo, company address, ingredient panel) rather than name alone.[6][7][8][9][10][11][12][13]

### How to confirm your specific bottle

To be sure which Omega Max you have and whether it’s the Fenix/Accentrate brain‑targeted formula or a different one:

- Check the **company name** and URL on the label (e.g., “Fenix Health Science” or “Accentrate” vs “Phoenix Health,” pharmacy name, or another brand).  
- Look for **LPC‑EPA / LPC‑DHA / LYSOVETA** in the ingredient list if it’s the Accentrate Omega Max brain‑focused product.[4]
