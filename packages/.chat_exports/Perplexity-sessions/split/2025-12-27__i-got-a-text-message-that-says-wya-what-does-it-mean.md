## Human

I got a text message that says Wya.  what does it mean?

## Assistant

“Wya” is shorthand for **“where you at?”**—a casual way of asking where you are or what you’re up to.[1][2]

## Main meaning

- Most of the time, it literally means “Where are you?” in texts, Snapchat, etc.[3][4][1]
- It can also loosely mean “What are you doing / why aren’t you answering?” depending on context.[5][3]

## How to respond

- If it’s about location: reply with where you are (e.g., “At home,” “On my way,” “Just got to the mall”).[1][3]
- If it feels flirty or social, they may be hinting at hanging out or meeting up, so you can say whether you’re free or not.[6][3][1]
