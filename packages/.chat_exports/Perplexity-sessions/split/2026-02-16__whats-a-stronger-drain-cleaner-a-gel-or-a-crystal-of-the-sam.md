## Human

What's a stronger drain cleaner, a gel or a crystal of the same brand?

## Assistant

Crystal drain cleaners are generally stronger than gel versions from the same brand for tackling tough clogs.[1]

## Key Differences
Crystal (or granular) cleaners, often pure sodium hydroxide (lye), generate intense heat and deeply penetrate clogs when water is added, making them highly effective for grease, hair, and organic buildup.  Gels provide better pipe adhesion for extended contact but act more slowly with less aggressive reactions.[2][1]

## Effectiveness Comparison
| Aspect          | Crystal/Granular                  | Gel                              |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Strength       | Higher; concentrated caustic power [1] | Moderate; thicker but diluted [2] |
| Penetration    | Deep into pipes [1]           | Clings to walls, limited reach [2] |
| Best For       | Stubborn, deep clogs [1]     | Surface/vertical clogs [2]   |

Use crystals cautiously—they're more corrosive to pipes and skin.[1]
