## Human

I have a kitchen island that I want to use polished concrete for the countertop. 4 feet by 10 feet. How expensive will that be in Calgary and is that a good or a bad idea?

## Assistant

Polished concrete countertops for a 4x10-foot (40 sq ft) kitchen island in Calgary typically cost $135–$295 per square foot installed, leading to a total estimate of $5,400–$11,800 depending on finish, edges, and custom features. Local contractors like Elevation Renovations quote standard polished options at $175–$225 per sq ft for islands. This positions it as a premium choice compared to quartz ($70–$120/sq ft) or granite ($60–$110/sq ft).[1][2][3][4]

## Cost Breakdown
Expect these factors to influence the final price for your 40 sq ft island:
- **Basic polished**: $135–$175/sq ft ($5,400–$7,000 total).[1]
- **Premium/artistic (e.g., inlays, custom edges)**: $175–$295/sq ft ($7,000–$11,800).[5][1]
- **Additional**: Sealing ($200–$500), reinforcement for spans, or integrated sinks add 10–20%.[5]
Prices reflect Calgary's market in 2026, with labor at $45–$135/sq ft due to custom casting and polishing.[3]

## Pros and Cons
Polished concrete offers unique appeal but requires careful consideration.

| Aspect | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| Durability | Heat-resistant, handles heavy loads on islands; engineered for Calgary's freeze-thaw cycles.[3] | Porous if unsealed; stains from wine/acids possible without regular maintenance.[6][7] |
| Aesthetics | Custom colors/textures, seamless large spans (ideal for 10-ft island), develops patina.[1] | Shows etching from hot pans if not protected; needs resealing every 1–2 years.[7] |
| Practicality | Warm tactile feel, integrates sinks/lighting. | Higher upkeep than quartz; potential cracking if cabinets shift.[5] |

## Recommendation
Opt for polished concrete if you prioritize a custom, industrial look and commit to sealing—it's a good idea for durable, unique islands from reputable Calgary firms like Elevation or Calgary Concrete Services. Otherwise, quartz provides similar style with less maintenance at lower cost. Get 3 local quotes for accuracy.[2][8][1]
