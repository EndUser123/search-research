## Assistant

TubeOnAI is an AI-powered summarization and transcription tool that lets you quickly extract key insights, transcripts, and repurposed content from YouTube videos, podcasts, articles, PDFs, and other sources via web app and browser/mobile apps.[1][2][3]

## What TubeOnAI Does

- Provides **summaries** of long-form content (YouTube, podcasts, web pages, PDFs, documents, audio) so you can get key takeaways in minutes.[2][3][1]
- Automatically retrieves or generates a **transcript**, then summarizes it, giving you both transcript and summary for YouTube videos.[4][3]
- Supports **content repurposing**, turning one video or document into blog posts, social posts, scripts, and other formats.[5][1][4]

## Platforms and Integrations

- Available as: web app, Chrome extension, iOS app, and Android app, with sync across devices.[6][7][8][2]
- The Chrome extension adds a widget on YouTube and web pages so you can trigger summaries, transcripts, translations, and timestamps in-page.[8][2]

## Notable Features

- Multi-format support: YouTube, Spotify/Apple podcasts, articles, PDFs, MP3/MP4, and other uploads.[3][9][2]
- Free tier: summary, translation, transcript, and timestamps from the extension; paid plans unlock chat-style Q&A, audio playback, repurposing templates, and custom prompts.[9][8][4]
- Uses advanced NLP models to go beyond plain transcription by extracting **key insights** and structure, which reviews position as an upgrade over basic transcript-only tools.[3][9]

## How It Works (YouTube Example)

- You open a YouTube video; the TubeOnAI widget appears via the Chrome extension.[8][2]
- You log in and click “Summarize”; it fetches or generates the transcript, then produces a structured summary and optionally other formats.[4][8]
