## Human

Show me a picture of an E20 bulb for a Himalayan salt lamp

## Assistant

An E20 bulb refers to a European small screw (SES) base with a 20mm diameter, similar to E17 but less common; however, no direct matches for E20 in Himalayan salt lamps appear in current sources, which overwhelmingly specify E12 candelabra bases (much smaller at 12mm) for these lamps.[1][2][3]
## Typical Bulb
Himalayan salt lamps standardly use 15-25W incandescent E12/T20-shaped bulbs to produce gentle heat for ion effects.[4][5][2]
