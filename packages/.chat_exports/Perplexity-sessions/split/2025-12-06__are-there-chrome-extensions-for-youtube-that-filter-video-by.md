## Human

are there chrome extensions for youtube that filter video by date?

## Assistant

Yes. There are several Chrome extensions that add more powerful date filters for YouTube than the built‑in UI.[1][2]

## Notable extensions

- **Hush Feed** – Lets you filter YouTube videos on the page by publish date, duration, view count, and keywords, giving you finer control over which uploads you see.[2]
- **YouTube Eye: Find popular (month, year, etc)** – Focused on channels: you can see a channel’s most popular videos within a chosen date range (past week, month, year, or custom).[3]
- **YT YouFilter – YT Advanced Search Filter** – Adds advanced filtering and sorting options to YouTube search results, including upload date in combination with other criteria.[4][5]

## Built‑in vs extensions

YouTube’s native filters only let you restrict by coarse ranges like “Last hour”, “Today”, “This week”, “This month”, or “This year”, and do not support arbitrary date ranges.  The extensions above help when you need more precise ranges (for example, “only 2022 videos” or “between two dates”) or want date filters while browsing a channel or feed instead of just search results.[6][5][3][1][2]
