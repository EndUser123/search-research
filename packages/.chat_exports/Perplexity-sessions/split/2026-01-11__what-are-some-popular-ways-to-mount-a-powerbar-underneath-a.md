## Human

what are some popular ways to mount a powerbar underneath a table?

## Assistant

Popular ways to mount a powerbar under a table include using screws through built-in holes, heavy-duty adhesive tapes or strips, and dedicated holders or trays. These methods help organize cables and keep the workspace tidy while ensuring accessibility and safety.[1][2][3]

## Screw Mounting
Many powerbars feature rear notches or holes designed for screws. Measure the spacing, drive wood screws loosely into the table underside, slide the powerbar onto them, and tighten for a secure fit.  This permanent method works well on wooden desks and avoids adhesives.[4][2][3][5][1]

## Adhesive Methods
Heavy-duty double-sided tape like 3M VHB or Command strips provides no-drill installation. Apply tape to the powerbar back, press firmly against the table for 30-60 seconds, and allow 24 hours to cure before use.  Users recommend clamping during setting for better adhesion on heavier units.[6][3][7][8]

## Holders and Trays
Dedicated plastic or metal brackets mount via adhesive or screws and cradle the powerbar.  Wire or ventilated trays clamp or screw under the desk to hold the powerbar plus adapters, preventing heat buildup.  3D-printed custom holders are also popular for precise fits.[9][8][10][11][1]

## Human

How to mount a power strip without drilling into desk

## Assistant

Mounting a power strip under a desk without drilling uses adhesives, clamps, or hooks for secure, damage-free installation. These no-drill methods prioritize strong adhesion on clean surfaces and allow easy removal. Heavy-duty options support typical power strip weights up to several pounds.[1][2]

## Adhesive Tape or Pads
Clean the desk underside thoroughly, then apply heavy-duty double-sided tape like 3M VHB or included pads (e.g., on Anker models) to the power strip's back. Press firmly for 30-60 seconds and wait 24 hours before plugging in devices.  This works on smooth wood, laminate, or metal but test weight capacity first.[2][3]

## Command Strips or Hooks
Attach 3M Command strips or heavy-duty hooks rated for 5+ pounds to the desk and power strip. Align, press to engage, and follow hold time instructions (often 1 hour).  Hooks allow swinging access for easier plugging.[4][5][1]

## Clamp-On Trays or Holders
Use no-drill clamp trays that grip desk edges or legs, sliding the power strip inside a ventilated holder.  Adhesive-backed brackets from retailers like Etsy or Amazon.ca cradle the strip without tools.  Velcro straps or zip ties looped around legs provide quick, adjustable setups.[6][7][8][9][10][1]
