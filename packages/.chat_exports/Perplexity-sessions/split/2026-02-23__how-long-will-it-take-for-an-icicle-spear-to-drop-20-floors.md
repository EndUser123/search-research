## Human

How long will it take for an icicle spear to drop 20 floors?

## Assistant

Assuming the icicle simply falls under gravity and you mean a typical building, it will take on the order of 2–3 seconds to drop 20 floors.

### Estimating the time

- A common rough height per floor is about 3–3.5 meters for standard multi‑story buildings.[1][2][3]  
- For 20 floors, that’s roughly $$20 \times 3$$ to $$20 \times 3.5 \approx 60$$–70 meters of height.[1][2][3]  
- For an object dropped from rest and ignoring air resistance, the free‑fall time from height $$h$$ is $$t = \sqrt{2h/g}$$, where $$g \approx 9.8 \text{ m/s}^2$$.[4][5][6]  

Using 60–70 m in that formula:

- From 60 m: $$t \approx \sqrt{2 \times 60 / 9.8} \approx 3.5$$ seconds.[4][5][6]  
- From 70 m: $$t \approx \sqrt{2 \times 70 / 9.8} \approx 3.8$$ seconds.[4][5][6]  

An icicle has drag, so air resistance will slow it somewhat and make the real time a bit longer than the ideal calculation.[7][8] In practice, you’re looking at roughly 3–4 seconds from a 20‑floor height, depending on the exact floor height, shape of the icicle, and wind.
