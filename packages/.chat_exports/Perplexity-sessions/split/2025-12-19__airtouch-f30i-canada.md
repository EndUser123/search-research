## Assistant

The AirTouch F30i is a full-face CPAP mask from ResMed, featuring a soft ComfiSoft fabric cushion with AdaptiSeal technology for a secure, adaptive seal that minimizes red marks and pressure points.[1][3]

## Key Features
It includes a top-of-head tube connection with 360° rotation for freedom of movement, QuietAir venting for reduced noise (rated quiet by over 90% of users), and magnetic clips for easy on/off.[2][1]

## Availability in Canada
Canadian retailers like MyCPAPCanada.ca, Airvoel.ca, CPAPOutlet.ca, and CPAPMachinesCanada.ca offer it for pre-order or purchase, often with free shipping on orders over $150 CAD; prices start around $236–$285 CAD depending on size and bundle.[5][7][1]

## Sizing and Care
Use ResMed's sizing guide for small, medium, or large cushions; replace cushions every 2 weeks, frames every 3 months, and headgear every 6 months. Clean daily with mild soap and warm water, avoiding ozone/UV cleaners.[3][1]

## Recall Note
Older AirFit F30i models were part of a 2023 Health Canada recall for potential foam degradation—verify your unit's model/serial with ResMed or retailers for the newer AirTouch F30i.[8]
