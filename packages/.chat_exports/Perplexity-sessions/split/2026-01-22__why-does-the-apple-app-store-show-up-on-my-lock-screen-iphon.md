## Human

why does the apple app store show up on my lock screen iphone

## Assistant

The App Store icon or prompts on your iPhone lock screen are usually just notifications or “suggested apps,” not that the App Store is actually running by itself.[1][2][3]

## Main reasons it appears

- **App Store notifications**: iOS can show App Store alerts (updates, recommendations, subscriptions, etc.) on the lock screen if notifications are enabled for App Store.[2][1]
- **Suggested app icon**: iOS has a “Suggested Apps” feature that can show an app icon (including App Store) at the bottom-left of the lock screen based on location or other context.[4]
- **Widgets/shortcuts**: Some lock-screen customization apps or widgets (e.g., app launchers) can put an App Store shortcut on the lock screen.[5][3]

## How to stop seeing it

Try these in order:

1. **Turn off App Store lock-screen notifications**  
   - Go to **Settings → App Store → Notifications**.  
   - Under **Alerts**, turn off **Lock Screen**.[1]

2. **Adjust general notification style**  
   - Go to **Settings → Notifications → Display As** and choose how notifications appear (Count/Stack/List) if they are cluttering the lock screen.[2]

3. **Disable Suggested Apps (if it’s a corner icon)**  
   - Go to **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → System Services**.  
   - Look for options like **Location-Based Suggestions** and turn them off; this can stop suggested app icons on the lock screen.[4]

4. **Check Lock Screen widgets/shortcuts**  
   - Long-press your lock screen → **Customize**.  
   - Remove any widget or shortcut that is set to open the App Store.[3][5]

If you can describe what it looks like (e.g., small icon in bottom corner, banner in middle, widget at top) or send a screenshot, more precise steps can be given.

## Human

my phone is on the long side in a stand.  the screen has two panels, the left is an analog clock, the right is an add for an apple store app.

## Assistant

Your iPhone is in **StandBy mode**, a built-in iOS feature (iOS 17+) that activates automatically when the phone is charging, placed on its side (landscape orientation) in a stand, and left stationary—turning it into a smart display like a bedside clock.  The screen splits into two panels: the left shows clocks (including analog styles), and the right displays widgets, where the App Store widget is appearing as an "ad" for featured apps or stories.[1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Why the App Store "ad" shows up
StandBy defaults to certain widgets like App Store on the right panel, which highlights app recommendations or promotions—many users call it an ad since it's promotional content from Apple.[3][5][1]
It can auto-suggest or prioritize App Store if widget suggestions are enabled or if it's in your stack.[7][5]
Analog clock on the left is one of the customizable clock views you swipe to access.[8][6]

## Remove the App Store widget
While in StandBy (phone charging on side):
1. Swipe left/right to the **widgets view** (right panel active).  
2. Touch and hold the right panel (App Store widget).  
3. Swipe up/down in the stack to find App Store, tap the **– (minus)** button, then **Remove App Store Widget**.[3]
4. Tap **Done** or add a preferred widget (e.g., Weather, Calendar) via the **+** button.[2][3]

## Customize further
- **Exit StandBy temporarily**: Pick up the phone, lay it flat, unplug, or press side button.  
- **Global settings**: Go to **Settings > StandBy** to toggle auto-activation or night mode. Turn off **Widget Suggestions** if available to prevent auto-adds.[6]
- For more clocks/widgets: Swipe up/down on left/right panels; long-press to edit stacks and add from your apps (e.g., analog clocks from Clock app or third-party).[2][6]

This setup is ideal for your stand use case but fully customizable to ditch the App Store promo.[6][3]
