## Human

Give me 3 ideas of one-pan meals I can make for dinner tonight

## Assistant

Here are three simple one-pan meal ideas ready in under an hour using common ingredients.[2][5]

## Sheet Pan Chicken Thighs
Toss chicken thighs, potatoes, Brussels sprouts, and bacon in one sheet pan, seasoning with salt, pepper, and herbs. Roast at 425°F for 35-40 minutes until crispy and tender, letting bacon fat flavor everything.[2]

## Lemon Parmesan Chicken and Rice
Sauté chicken in a skillet, add rice, broth, lemon juice, and Parmesan, then simmer covered for 20 minutes until creamy and infused with citrus notes.[2]

## One Skillet Mexican Quinoa
Cook ground beef or turkey with onions, garlic, quinoa, black beans, corn, tomatoes, and spices like cumin and chili powder in one pan for 25 minutes, topping with avocado if desired.[7][2]
