## Human

Cool lime lemonade refresher.  Starbucks.  How can my wife make it at home?

## Assistant

Your wife can get very close to the Starbucks Cool Lime Lemonade Refresher with a simple lime–mint–cucumber base plus lemonade and lots of ice.[1][2][3][4]

## What Starbucks is actually doing

- Starbucks describes the drink as “real fruit juice and mint and lime-slice flavours balanced with the delightful zing of lemonade.” **Fact.**[1]
- Consensus among copycat recipes is that the “cool” note comes from mint and sometimes cucumber infused in water, plus plenty of lime juice and sugar/sweetener. **Consensus.**[2][3][4][5]
- Some barista and fan reconstructions also add white grape juice to mimic the sweetness and body of the original refresher base. **Consensus.**[4][6]

So the key components are:
- Lime juice (fresh)
- Mint
- Optional cucumber
- Sweetener (sugar or alternative)
- Water + lemonade
- Ice and lime slices

## Practical home recipe (no weird ingredients)

This version avoids coffee powders and specialty syrups and uses things you can grab at a normal grocery store.[3][2][4]

### Step 1 – Make a “cool lime” base

For about 4 tall glasses:

- 3 cups cold water[7][2]
- 2 cups freshly squeezed lime juice (8–10 limes depending on size)[5][2][7]
- ¼ cup fresh mint leaves, finely chopped[2][7]
- ¾ cup cucumber, peeled and finely chopped (optional but helps with the “cool” profile)[3][4][7][2]
- ¾ cup sugar or equivalent sweetener, adjust to taste[4][5][7][2]

Steps:

1. Finely chop mint and cucumber so they infuse quickly into the water.[2][3][4]
2. Add mint and cucumber to the 3 cups of water in a jug or lidded container and let them sit at least 15–20 minutes; overnight in the fridge gives a stronger flavor.[3][4][2]
3. Strain out the mint and cucumber (or lift out the infuser if your pitcher has one), keeping the infused water.[4][2][3]
4. Stir in the lime juice and sugar/sweetener until dissolved.[5][7][2][4]

You now have a concentrated “cool lime” base: minty, lime-forward, slightly cucumber-cool.

### Step 2 – Turn it into the lemonade refresher

To serve one glass:

- Fill a glass with ice.
- Add ½ glass of your cool lime base.
- Top with store-bought still lemonade (or homemade lemonade) to taste, usually another ¼–½ glass, and stir.[8][1]
- Add a lime slice and a few mint leaves on top.[8][2][3]

Taste and tweak:
- Too sharp → add more lemonade or a bit more sugar.
- Too sweet → add more plain cold water or extra lime juice.
- Not “cool” enough → next batch, increase mint and cucumber or infuse overnight.[2][3][4]

## If she wants to get closer to the original base

Several copycats try to mimic Starbucks’ prepared refresher base that includes grape juice. This is overkill for everyday use but worth knowing.[6][4]

A more “authentic” base can be:

- 1 cup peeled, diced cucumber + ½ cup chopped mint, infused in 3 cups water for 2–24 hours, then strained.[4]
- 1 cup sugar dissolved in 1 cup water as a simple syrup, optionally with a small amount of coffee extract (Green Bean coffee powder) for the very faint caffeine/tea note.[4]
- 1½ cups fresh lime juice plus 1 cup white grape juice and an extra cup of cold water mixed into the infused water and simple syrup.[4]

This yields a large batch resembling the commercial refresher base that you can store in the fridge and shake with ice and lemonade when serving.[4]

## Quick “lazy” version (no infusing, 2 minutes work)

If she wants something very fast that still hits the same general vibe:

- ½ glass good-quality still lemonade.[8]
- Juice of half a lime.
- A small handful of mint leaves torn by hand.
- Optional: a few thin cucumber slices.
- Top with cold water or mild sparkling water, then ice and a lime slice.[9][10][11][12]

This won’t be as layered as an infused base but is highly drinkable and easy for daily use.[9][8]
