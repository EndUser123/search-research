## Human

The dark black scenes on my TV are too black. I can't see anything. How can I fix it?

## Assistant

**Crushed blacks on your TV, where dark scenes lose detail, often result from incorrect picture settings like low brightness or gamma, or features like HDR overpowering the image.** Adjusting these can reveal shadow details without washing out true blacks.[1][2]

## Quick Fixes
- **Darken the room**: Reduce ambient light or glare, as it makes blacks look lifted and details invisible—draw curtains and dim lamps.[3][4]
- **Disable auto features**: Turn off light sensors or eco modes that dim the screen automatically.[3]
- **Switch picture mode**: Use "Movie," "Cinema," or "Filmmaker Mode" instead of "Vivid" or "Standard," which crush shadows.[5][6]

## Key Settings Adjustments
Pause on a dark scene and tweak these (note original values first):
- Increase **Brightness** slightly until black bars show faint detail (avoid overdoing to keep deep blacks).[6][2]
- Lower **Gamma** to 2.0–2.1 (from default 2.2–2.4) for better shadow visibility.[7][1]
- Boost **Backlight/OLED Light** and set **Local Dimming** to Medium/High.[1]
- Enable **Dynamic Contrast** if manual tweaks feel fiddly.[4]

| Setting | Typical Fix | Why It Helps |
|---------|-------------|--------------|
| Brightness | +5–10 from min | Lifts shadows without graying blacks [2] |
| Gamma | 2.0–2.1 | Smooths dark-to-midtone transition [7] |
| Contrast | 45–50/100 | Enhances light/dark difference [8] |
| Black Level/Shadow Detail | +5 (e.g., LG 50→56) | Restores near-black details [9] |

## Calibration Test
Search YouTube for "black level test pattern 0-255" or "near black test." Play it, adjust brightness/gamma until you see subtle gray steps above pure black without clipping whites.[10][2]

## Advanced Tips
For HDR content, toggle HDR off temporarily or adjust HDR-specific black levels. Brand-specific: Samsung "Shadow Detail," Sony "Black Equalizer" to High, LG "Black Level." If issues persist, check input (e.g., HDMI black level mismatch) or reset to defaults.[11][12][13][7][3]
