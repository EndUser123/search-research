## Human

Are there grounding mats for mice and keyboard

## Assistant

Yes, there are grounding mats and pads specifically sized for mouse and keyboard use, and larger desk mats that can sit under both.

## Types of grounding mats

- **Earthing/health-focused desk mats**: Sold as “grounding mats” or “earthing mats” and marketed for wellness. They are often long enough to sit under a keyboard and mouse, and connect to the ground pin of a wall outlet via a cord.[1][2]
- **Grounding mouse pads**: Smaller pads that replace a normal mousepad and ground your hand/forearm while using the mouse.[3][4]
- **ESD/anti-static desk mats**: Designed for electronics and static control, not “earthing wellness,” but they do ground the work surface and can cover keyboard and mouse areas.[5][6][7][8][9]

## Options that fit keyboard + mouse

- Universal earthing mats (around 68 cm x 25 cm / 27" x 10") are explicitly advertised as being usable under a keyboard and mouse so your hands/wrists rest on the conductive surface while typing or mousing.[1]
- Large grounding desk mats are sold as full-width desk covers; manufacturers state they can hold computer, keyboard, mouse, and other tools while remaining grounded.[2]
- Some anti‑static table mats are marketed specifically “for keyboard and mouse,” with appropriate surface resistivity and included ground straps.[8][9]

## Safety and setup notes

- These mats typically connect only to the **ground** of an outlet via a dedicated cord, not to live or neutral, which is standard practice for both earthing and ESD mats.[6][3][2]
- For health-style earthing mats, some skin contact is required; they work best when your hands/wrists or bare feet touch the conductive surface directly or through thin fabric.[2][1]
- ESD/anti-static mats for electronics often include a snap and ground cord and may specify compliance with standards like ANSI/ESD S4.1 for surface resistance.[7][5][6]
