## Assistant

Comet Browser supports vertical tabs via a settings option, allowing better organization especially on wide screens. This feature moves the tab bar from the top to the left or right side.[1]

## Enable Vertical Tabs
Navigate to Settings → Appearance → Tab Layout and select “Vertical (Left)” or “Vertical (Right)”. This doubles visible tabs and suits ultrawide monitors.[1]

## Benefits
Vertical layout improves tab management by showing more titles without scrolling. Hover previews provide thumbnails, AI summaries, and memory usage for quick reference.[1]

Related features include AI tab grouping (Cmd+Shift+G) and tab stacking for further organization.[1]
