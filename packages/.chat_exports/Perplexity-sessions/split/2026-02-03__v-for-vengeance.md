## Film Overview
Two estranged "good" vampire sisters, Emma and Scarlett, reunite to rescue their kidnapped younger sister Kate from a gang of "bad" vampires led by Thorn, who killed their parents and seeks a vampirism vaccine. The story blends family drama, supernatural battles, and thriller elements, with the sisters teaming up alongside a bounty hunter named Marcus. Directed by Kelly Hailan, it stars Jocelyn Hudon, Grace Van Dien, and Sean Maguire, running 86 minutes with an R rating.[2][4][7][1]
