## Human

What did Trump say today about the Strait of Hormuz and coming to get your oil?

## Assistant

Trump has, over the last 24–48 hours, basically said that keeping the Strait of Hormuz open is no longer primarily a U.S. responsibility and that other countries should secure their own energy and “go get” their oil themselves.[1][2][3]

### What he actually said

Today and in posts and interviews referenced in today’s coverage, Trump has been quoted saying:

- Securing the Strait of Hormuz is “not for us,” estimating the U.S. will be done with major attacks in roughly two to three weeks.[3][1]
- U.S. allies facing high fuel prices should “go get your own oil,” and “learn how to fight for yourself,” directed especially at European partners like the U.K. and France.[4][2][5][3]
- In a Truth Social post, he told allies to “go to the Strait, and just TAKE IT,” saying “the U.S.A. won’t be there to help you anymore,” in the context of Iran’s disruption of traffic through Hormuz.[6][2]

So the “coming to get your oil” idea you’re referencing corresponds to this cluster of lines where he tells other countries to go to Hormuz and “take” or “get” their own oil, and signals that the U.S. will not keep guaranteeing security for that chokepoint in the way it has historically done.[7][2][4][6][3]
