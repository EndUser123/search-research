## Human

in calgary, what temp should I use the oven to reheat pizza?

## Assistant

To reheat pizza in Calgary's cold winter climate, use a moderate oven temperature to ensure even crisping without drying it out. The location doesn't alter the method, as oven performance is consistent indoors.

## Recommended Temperature
Set your oven to 375°F (190°C). This strikes a balance for melting cheese while reviving the crust's crispiness.[1][5][7][8][9]

## Step-by-Step Method
- Preheat the oven fully (10-15 minutes) with a baking sheet or pizza stone inside.
- Place cold pizza slices directly on the hot sheet (or foil-lined for easy cleanup).
- Heat for 8-12 minutes, checking at 8 minutes—the cheese should bubble and crust crisp.[6][12]

## Quick Tips
- For extra crispiness, start with a cold oven at 425°F and heat until it beeps (about 10 minutes).[2]
- Avoid microwaves, as they make crust soggy; ovens outperform them here.[3]
