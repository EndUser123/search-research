## Human

Why does Canada say us health is no longer a trustworthy partner

## Assistant

Canada's Health Minister Marjorie Michel stated that U.S. health institutions are no longer trustworthy partners due to recent changes under President Donald Trump's administration. This shift stems from actions like budget cuts to key agencies and the promotion of vaccine misinformation.[1][2][3]

## Key Reasons
U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) updated its website in late November 2025 to claim vaccines do not cause autism in a way that contradicts established science, eroding trust. Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr., an anti-vaccine activist, appointed an advisory panel recommending against routine hepatitis B vaccines for newborns and reviewing childhood schedules. Budget slashes to CDC and National Institutes of Health have impaired data collection and public health efforts.[3][4][1]

## Canadian Response
Michel emphasized Canada must seek partnerships with science-aligned countries, especially on vaccines amid a measles outbreak that ended Canada's measles-free status. Provinces agreed in October 2025 to prioritize vaccination to rebuild trust post-COVID misinformation. Canadian experts warn U.S. politicization contributes to North American disease rises, like measles in Ontario and Alberta.[5][1]
