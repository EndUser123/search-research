## Human

On my iPhone, after upgrading the operating system, I now see ads on my lock screen when the phone is sideways. How do I stop those ads?

## Assistant

The ads appearing on your iPhone lock screen in landscape (sideways) orientation after an OS upgrade are most likely notifications from apps, promotional content from Apple services, or elements in StandBy mode (which activates when charging sideways). Apple does not display third-party ads directly on the lock screen, but user reports confirm these can mimic ads. Disabling them involves checking notifications and StandBy settings.[1][2][3][4][5]

## Manage Notifications
Go to Settings > Notifications, select suspect apps (e.g., recently installed or promotional ones), and turn off "Allow Notifications" or set "Show Previews" to Never for lock screen privacy. For Apple-specific promotions, check Settings > Notifications > Apple Wallet or News and disable if present.[6][4][7][1]

## Disable StandBy Mode
StandBy triggers full-screen widgets, clocks, or photos when sideways and charging, which post-upgrade users often mistake for ads. Open Settings > StandBy and toggle it off completely. Customize or limit it via options like "Nightstand" if you want partial use.[8][2][3][5]

## Additional Fixes
Turn off Personalized Ads at Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising. Restart your iPhone (quick Volume Up, Volume Down, hold Side button) to clear glitches, and ensure no new apps push lock screen content. If persistent, update iOS and apps via Settings > General > Software Update.[9][3][1]
