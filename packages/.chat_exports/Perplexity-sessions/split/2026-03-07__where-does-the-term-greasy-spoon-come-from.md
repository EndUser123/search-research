## Human

Where does the term "greasy spoon" come from?

## Assistant

**"Greasy spoon"** refers to a small, inexpensive diner or cafe known for hearty, fried foods like fry-ups.[1]

## Origin
The term emerged in the United States in the early 20th century, with the earliest printed use in 1906 in *Macmillan's Magazine*. It described a simple Paris eatery frequented by Robert Louis Stevenson around 1874, evoking utensils greasy from heavy frying.[3][1]

The concept traces back further to 1848, when a murky Rome restaurant was dubbed the "Dirty Spoon" by Germans. By the 1920s, "greasy spoon" gained popularity in American slang for cheap, no-frills spots.[4][1][3]

## Usage
It's now common across English-speaking countries like the UK (often with "caff") for divey diners serving comfort food. Early U.S. examples from 1891–1898 in Minnesota papers named specific greasy spots "The Greasy Spoon."[1][4]
