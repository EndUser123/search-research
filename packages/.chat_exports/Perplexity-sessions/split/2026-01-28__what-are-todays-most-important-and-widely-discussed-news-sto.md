Today's top news stories center on escalating U.S. immigration enforcement controversies in Minneapolis, a fatal plane crash in India involving a top politician, and an attack on Rep. Ilhan Omar.[1][2][3]

## Minneapolis ICE Crackdown
Federal agents, including ICE and Border Patrol, are facing intense backlash over the fatal shooting of U.S. citizen Alex Pretti on January 24 during protests against immigration raids. Bystander videos contradict official claims that Pretti was armed, showing him holding a phone while shielding a woman from agents; two agents fired, killing him, the second such incident this month. Protests continue amid reports of ICE agents attempting to enter the Ecuadorian consulate and viral videos of overcrowded holding rooms, with Trump pledging de-escalation while sending more enforcers.[4][2][5][6][7][8][9][10][11]

## Attack on Ilhan Omar
Rep. Ilhan Omar (D-Minn.) was sprayed with a strong-smelling liquid from a syringe by a man at a Minneapolis town hall on Tuesday, just before she called for abolishing ICE. The attacker, 55-year-old Anthony Kazmierczak, was arrested on assault charges; Omar, unharmed, continued the event amid applause. The incident follows President Trump's mockery of her at a rally and another assault on a Democratic congressmember.[12][13][14][1]

## India Plane Crash
Maharashtra Deputy Chief Minister Ajit Pawar and four others died when their Learjet 45 crashed and exploded at Baramati Airport during a landing attempt on Wednesday morning. Preliminary reports cite technical failure on the second approach; Pawar was en route to political events. A state funeral is planned, marking a major loss for India's NCP party.[15][3][16][17][18]
