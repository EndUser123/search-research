## Assistant

Art of Problem Solving (AoPS) is a math education program and online community designed for students who want to go deeper and more rigorous than standard school math, especially those interested in contests like AMC, AIME, and math olympiads.[1][6]

## What AoPS Is

- AoPS is a company that offers advanced math textbooks, online classes, and a very active problem-solving community focused on grades roughly 5–12.[2][1]
- Its curriculum emphasizes **problem** solving and conceptual understanding over memorizing formulas or following rote procedures.[5][1]

## Main Components

- Textbooks: A full series covering prealgebra through calculus, plus topics like counting & probability, number theory, and geometry, all written in a problem-first style.[2][5]
- Online School: Live, instructor-led classes with homework, message boards, and contest-prep courses for things like AMC, AIME, and beyond.[8][2]
- Community & Tools: Forums, an adaptive practice system (Alcumus), free videos, and a wiki with contest problem archives and solutions.[10][1]

## Who It’s For

- Motivated students who enjoy challenging problems, including contest-oriented and “mathy” kids looking for more depth than school.[6][2]
- Often recommended for strong or gifted students; many find it significantly harder than typical school curricula, which is intentional to build resilience and higher-level reasoning.[7][5]

## Teaching Style

- Problems typically come first: students experiment on hard problems, then see explanations and strategies derived from those problems.[9][5]
- The materials focus on building flexible strategies, multiple solution paths, and mathematical thinking, rather than just teaching “recipes” for standard exercises.[5][9]
