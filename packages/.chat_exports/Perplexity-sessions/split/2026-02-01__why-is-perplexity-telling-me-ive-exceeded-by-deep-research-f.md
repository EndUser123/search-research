## Human

Why is perplexity telling me I've exceeded by deep research for the month?

## Assistant

Perplexity shows that “Deep Research limit exceeded” message when you’ve used up the number of Research queries included in your plan’s monthly quota.[1]

### What “Deep Research” / Research is

- “Research queries” are the autonomous, multi-step in‑depth runs (Deep Research) that generate long, structured reports or do multi‑hop analysis, separate from normal Pro searches.[2][1]
- These have explicit monthly caps on all plans except certain high‑end enterprise tiers, even if basic and Pro searches still work.[3][1]

### Typical limits by plan

From the current plan comparison table:[1]

- Free: 1 Research query per month.[1]
- Pro / Education Pro / Max: A fixed monthly pool of Research queries (described as “Monthly limits (average use)” or “Monthly limits (advanced use)”). Exact numbers aren’t shown publicly per user but they are finite.[1]
- Enterprise Pro: “Extended limits (500/month)” for Research.[1]
- Enterprise Max: “Highest limits (1000/month)” for Research.[1]

Once you hit that number, additional Deep Research or Research runs for that billing month are blocked, which is why you see the message.[1]

### Why you might hit the limit unexpectedly

- Each time you trigger Deep Research (or a “Research” mode that runs an autonomous multi‑step investigation) it consumes one Research query, even if the prompt looks simple.[2]
- Some flows in the UI (e.g., buttons that say “Research” vs a normal search) always count against that pool.[2][1]
- The counter resets on your subscription billing cycle, not on the calendar month, so “for the month” refers to that period.[1]

### What you can do next

- Use normal Pro / advanced model searches instead of Deep Research until your period resets; those have separate, generally more generous limits.[4][1]
- If you truly need more Deep Research volume, upgrading to a higher plan (Max or Enterprise) increases monthly Research limits significantly.[3][1]
- If the warning appears and you’re sure you haven’t run that many Deep Research queries, contact Perplexity support via the help center so they can check your account’s counters.[1]

## Human

When's my 'month' reset?

## Assistant

Your “month” resets on your subscription billing cycle date, not on the calendar 1st.

- For example, if you subscribed on the 12th, your Deep Research quota will typically reset around the 12th of each month for that account’s plan.  
- To see the exact date for you, open your Perplexity account’s billing/subscription page; the renewal date shown there is the reset point for usage‑based monthly limits like Deep Research.
