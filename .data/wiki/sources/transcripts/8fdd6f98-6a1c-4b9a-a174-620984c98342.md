The 

shallow research agent

 runs a bounded tool-calling loop—up to 10 LLM turns and 5 tool calls—then returns a concise answer with citations. Simple questions like “What is CUDA?” resolve in seconds. The 

deep research agent

To keep all inference on-premise, change the orchestrator_llm to point at a self-hosted model.

Context management is central to how deep agents work. The planner agent produces a JSON research plan. The researcher agent receives only this plan — not the orchestrator's thinking 

tokens

https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/ai-tokens-explained/

reasoning

https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/glossary/ai-reasoning/

This ensures your research stack remains private, and deeply integrated with the data that matters most to your organization.
