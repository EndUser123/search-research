# The Upper Body Exercise Most Adults Neglect
push-ups are important but they only train half the equation To keep your shoulders feeling strong to improve your push-ups and to make everyday life feel a whole lot easier you also need to pull Today I'll show you the simplest way to train that the row Use a dumbbell or loaded backpack and pull the shoulder blade back first then your elbow up toward the sky Pause at the top position then slowly lower the weight back down Repeat till you get medium fatigue somewhere around 6 to 15 repetitions When you can do three sets of 15 at a given weight increase the weight by around 5 to 10 lbs If you can't do six repetitions in a set or you feel like you need to hold your breath grunt groan or compensate use less weight Even if that means using one pound or even zero pounds Don't shrug your shoulder toward your ear Don't crane your neck forward unconsciously Don't drop your chin into your chest to stare at your own crotch It's still there I promise Pair the row with your push-ups If you're doing push-up workouts two or three times a week then do two to three sets of these rows with them If you're using the strategy I mentioned in a previous video where you're just doing a few push-ups a day then just do one set of the rows at a light to medium level of challenge And no matter what schedule you're on make sure to do a few extra reps or a full extra set for your weaker side Let me know what schedule you're going to start with in the comments down below And then we need to talk about three things most people never think about The first is hand position I like to play with hand position in my rows In real life not everything has a perfectly sized handle that allows you to have a neutral grip Now neutral grip is when the palm is facing toward your body when you're doing the row I personally like having the thumb pointing in sometimes It really engages my back and shoulder muscles in a different way Or you can try having the thumb pointing out Find what feels like a good challenge for your body And when you feel more confident and strong you can use resistance that isn't a perfectly shaped weight in the gym It's not about right and wrong It's about making more positions feel right and strong Which is why you shouldn't skip the next option As modern humans have evolved into carbon-based screen holders the posterior shoulder has become horrendously atrophied The result is lost range of motion to the sides and up above parallel In a strong but very rational aversion to playing tugof-war with humans and dogs once a week do one or two sets of your horizontal pull with your elbow going way out to the side You will need to use much lighter weight This variation focuses the work on the rear deltoid and builds much needed strength in the posterior shoulder As with all new exercises please remember that slow is safe and fast is foolish Control the full range slowly If you load this too heavy or you are too aggressive you can hurt your shoulder So please be conservative And as you gain confidence in your row this third variable will help you become even more resilient for real life When you first start your rowing journey I strongly suggest getting your torso close to parallel with the floor You can do this with both feet on the floor and one hand supported or a knee and a hand on a bench or couch In the future you can be more upright or even tilted down to build strength and coordination in a different orientation This can be useful for building strength for pull-ups or for just picking heavy stuff up You can also use gymnastics rings or a suspension trainer to do this horizontal pulling motion with your body in a straight line with a totally different orientation The important thing is you're building strength in this pulling pattern pulling things towards your body or pulling your body towards an anchor point No matter how you do it the horizontal pull will help you discover new strength and confidence Your posture will improve and your push-ups will improve Maintaining balance in the muscles of the front of your shoulder and chest and the back of your shoulder will keep your shoulders healthy and strong for the long run And who knows one day you might win tug-of-war with your neighbor's husky Really as long as your arm is not getting ripped out of its socket you are winning Go to uprightalth.com/diy to build resilience
