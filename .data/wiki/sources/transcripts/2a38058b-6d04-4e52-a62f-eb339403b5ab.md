# Heartbreaking 💔 #adhd
i just received one of the most heartbreaking letters from a listener to my ADHD podcast and the line that really got me was "Hi Alex i wanted to share something very personal with you i tend to overshare when I'm speaking to new people because I feel like I'm considerably more tolerable with more context." That line got me because I know how common this feeling is for people with ADHD and I know where it comes from children with ADHD hear 20,000 extra micro rejections than a neurotypical child comments like "You're embarrassing yourself why are you being lazy stop biting your nails why are you crying stop being dramatic you're too sensitive you're rude stop being weird stop fidgeting calm down be normal." Even the nonverbal ones the sigh the eye roll the look and over time those moments teach a child something really dangerous that who they are naturally is wrong so they grow into adults who feel they need to explain themselves before they can be accepted adults who overexlain overshare mask people please apologize for existing and maybe the saddest part is this some people with ADHD spend their entire lives trying to be acceptable but to the person who sent me this letter I promise you who you really are is already
