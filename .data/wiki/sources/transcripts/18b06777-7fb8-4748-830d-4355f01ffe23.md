# Harness Engineering for AI agents #aistartup #aiagents #harnessengineering
anthropic and OpenAI are both doubling down on harness engineering so harness engineering has evolved from context engineering and context engineering has evolved from prompt engineering imagine your AI agent is an intern prompt engineering is telling your intern exactly what you want in a more detailed format if you want your intern to write an article you need to tell him how many words in what style it's about the topic on Pokemon and context engineering is giving your intern all of the right information at the right time for him to complete a task for example if he's writing an article about all of the 1,000 Pokémons if you give him all of the information up front he'll forget so if he's writing about a certain type of Pokémon you give him that part of the information that's rag that's one form of context engineering and then we have harness engineering that is ask your intern to carry out a long-term project and he needs to know that he's doing it right he needs to evaluate when he's finishing the project this could be you ask him to write a 50page paper on how playing Pokemon has impacted humans brain and because it's a multiple hours multiple days project he needs to have the structure to prompt himself to execute and then to evaluate his own work throughout that hours or days of work so that the whole 50page paper will make sense by the end and
