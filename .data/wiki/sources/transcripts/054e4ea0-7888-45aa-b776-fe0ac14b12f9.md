Domain & Purpose

The pre-mortem skill generates comprehensive failure analysis for any system, feature, or implementation by imagining "it's 6 months later and this failed — why?" The skill is critical because it counteracts optimism bias inherent in planning and implementation. It is invoked by solo developers to stress-test features before committing, to analyze failures during debugging, and to document risk patterns for future reference.

Key differentiator vs. risk assessment: traditional risk assessment asks "what MIGHT go wrong?" (filtered by optimism), while pre-mortem asks "it's 6 months later, it FAILED — why?" (liberates honest analysis).
