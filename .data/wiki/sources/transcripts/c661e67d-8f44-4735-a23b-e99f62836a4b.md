# How to Turn Your Weak Wrists Into... Deadly Weapons | Calisthenics Training(No Equipment)
did you know that the strongest muscles in your arm could become completely useless because of a tiny joint no larger than a few centimeters you could have massive forearms a crushing grip and even a punch capable of knocking a man down but if your wrist is weak all that power leaks out before it ever hits the target this is exactly why you see some fighters punch with all their might only to end up with hand or wrist pain while others can land hundreds of strikes without feeling a thing the secret isn't just in the muscles the secret is in the joints that support those muscles the wrist is athletes and professional fighters care just as much about strengthening their wrist as they do about building muscle today I'm going to show you five exercises that will make your wrist more solid body weight in the beginning you might not be able to do a full range of motion and that's perfectly fine start from your knees and progress gradually this exercise strengthens the small muscles inside the hand and the delicate tendons that traditional weights just can't reach over time you'll notice a massive increase in grip strength and wrist stability two knuckle push-ups fighters have used this exercise for decades instead of placing your palms flat on the floor support yourself on your knuckle keep your wrist perfectly straight and lower yourself slowly every single repetition forces the wrist to endure pressure in a way that directly mimics real punches it also improves force transfer from the arm to the fist and hardens the knuckles three reverse wrist stretch here we work on the side that everyone tends to neglect place the back of your hands on the floor with your fingers pointing toward your body then slowly rock your body backward and forward you'll feel a deep capacity to handle heavy loads and strengthens the ligaments that stabilize it five dynamic wrist rock place your hands on the floor and start shifting your body weight forward backward and from side
