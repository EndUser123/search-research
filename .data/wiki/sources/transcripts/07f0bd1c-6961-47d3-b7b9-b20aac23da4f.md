Disable Shared GPU Memory

To use GPU driver versions higher than 546.01, you need to disable the Sysmem Fallback Policy in the NVIDIA Control Center.

Open the NVIDIA Control Panel.

Select 

Manage 3D Settings

 from the 

3D Settings

 in the task bar.

Select 

CUDA - Sysmem Fallback Policy

.

Choose 

Prefer No Sysmem Fallback

 from the drop-down menu.

Click 

Apply

.
