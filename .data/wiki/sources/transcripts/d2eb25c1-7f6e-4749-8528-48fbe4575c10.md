Why This Model is the Best Choice

🔬 Unique Value Proposition

This is the ONLY uncensored model trained on DeepSeek-R1 reasoning traces.

Most reasoning models (o1, R1, etc.) have heavy safety guardrails. Most uncensored models lack advanced reasoning. 

Dolphin 3.0 R1 combines both

 — complex logic + unrestricted responses.
