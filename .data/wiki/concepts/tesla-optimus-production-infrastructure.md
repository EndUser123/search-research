## Operational details

- The AI5 chip, whose design was completed (taped out) by Elon Musk in late April 2026, is being built specifically for Optimus as a dedicated compute solution
- Tesla is preparing a new training infrastructure and expanding AI compute capabilities to support the robot's machine learning requirements
- The company plans to integrate Grock (xAI's language model) into Optimus's intelligence layer
- Production preparations include converting part of Fremont factory into an Optimus assembly line and planning a larger dedicated factory at Giga Texas
- The robot's hands are being redesigned for real-world task performance rather than purely demo purposes
- Tesla is applying the same full self-driving (FSD) philosophy that powers its vehicles to the robot's autonomous navigation capabilities
- The Gen 3 reveal delay signals Tesla is treating Optimus as a serious commercial product rather than a demonstration project
- An actuator company, speculated to be a supplier for both Tesla and competitor Figure AI, reported massive earnings indicating production ramp-up for humanoid robot components
- The V3 robot unveiling, originally scheduled for Q1 of the current year, appears to have been delayed
