## Operational details

- The platform offers 18 full-length training courses with over 300 lessons across the catalog, with one course containing 84 lessons totaling more than 8 hours of video content [source_6, source_9]
- Most courses provide a verifiable certificate upon completion, which can be added to LinkedIn profiles [source_9]
- Courses are taught by the engineers who built Claude, providing direct insight into model capabilities and intended usage [source_9]
- Course pages display four key pieces of information: the number of lessons, total hours of video, prerequisites, and difficulty level [source_6]
- The Claude Certified Architect (CCA-F) exam is the first official AI certification from a frontier model laboratory [source_2]
- Study strategies for certifications include using official study guides, practice exams, and additional preparation beyond provided materials [source_2]
- Claude 101 serves as the recommended starting point, covering core features including Projects, Artifacts, Skills, Connectors, Enterprise Search, Research mode, and different operating modes [source_9]
- Skills in Claude are discoverable through the 'find skills' capability, which searches the entire skill library and can install them automatically [source_4]
- The 'superpowers' skill prompts Claude to slow down, plan properly, and check its own work before executing tasks [source_4]
- Task Observer is a skill that monitors user work patterns, learns individual style, and continuously improves other skills in the background [source_4]
- Claude Co-work is a desktop-only feature that enables project-focused workflows and skill building through screen recording [source_3, source_8]
