# Ampersand Loop Knot for Drawstrings

## Decision context

**Definition:** The ampersand loop knot is a drawstring tying technique that forms an ampersand-shaped loop on one side of the drawstring and threads the other side through, around, and over to create a slip-resistant knot that maintains tension during physical activity.

## Operational details

- One hand forms an ampersand-shaped loop with one drawstring end while the other hand holds the opposing end
- The opposing end threads through the top of the loop, around the bottom, and pulls over the top before tightening
- The resulting knot resists loosening during movement and physical activity
- Tension can be adjusted after tying by gathering the loose end and thumb loop to add or release slack
- The knot resets to its secured state after tension adjustments without requiring full retying
- The technique works for sewn-in drawstrings by pulling tension on one side first before forming the loop
