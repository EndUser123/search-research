## Operational details

- Coffee machine manufacturers increasingly integrate multiple functions (grinding, brewing, steaming) into single devices, with source noting 'automated milk to screens of data on your app' as industry gimmicks
- Razor manufacturers design blades to degrade predictably—source identifies that 'the razor in your bathroom right now is not dull it just thinks it is' and that lifespan differences 'is not the steel not the coating not how often you shave'
- The razor-blade business model relies on high markup: 'a blade made of thin stainless steel that costs about 40 cents to manufacture' while consumers pay '$3 to $4 each'
- Coffee brand consolidation means competing brands may share ownership—source found 'brands you thought were competing secretly owned by the same company'
- Whole bean freshness is identified as critical: 'pre-ground coffee is basically stale bean dust in a fancy bag' with optimal flavor lasting 'about 15 minutes'
- Coffee machine design philosophies split between feature-rich all-in-one approaches and quality-focused single-function equipment
