## Operational details

- Border collies are described as a breed with inherent reactivity traits tied to their predatorial nature and high drive.
- One source demonstrates redirecting a reactive dog by offering an alternative activity—moving the dog to a different location or task when reactivity begins.
- Loose leash walking training is presented as a method to address leash reactivity, with emphasis on exact body positioning (leg placement, arm position, turning) rather than broad strokes.
- The approach claims effectiveness for fixed leash reactivity '99.9% of the time' using loose leash walking methods.
- Calming protocols before departure are described as targeting the dog's primitive brain with specific intentional signals to communicate safety.
- Sleeping proximity to owners is analyzed through pack animal behavior, where close sleeping represents survival instincts including safety, warmth, and security rather than purely affectionate behavior.
