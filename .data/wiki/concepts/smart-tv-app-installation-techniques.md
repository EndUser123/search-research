## Operational details

- Google TV and Android TV devices allow app installation through the Google Play Store by searching for desired apps and selecting the install option
- Fire TV devices utilize the Amazon App Store as the primary source, with some apps requiring alternative methods like the Downloader utility for accessing non-store content
- Some Fire TV installations involve entering specific numeric codes in the Downloader app to access hidden or unlinked store content
- Project TV Launcher serves as an alternative launcher for Google TV that removes default advertisements and provides more customization options
- Sideloading on Android TV and Fire Stick typically requires enabling developer options and using third-party installer applications
- TCL Google TV demonstrates the standard method of navigating to Apps section, using the search bar, and selecting apps from Google Play results
- Privacy settings such as Safe Search filters on Google TV can be configured to control content access across the platform
- VPN usage is recommended when installing third-party apps on streaming devices to protect user privacy and security
