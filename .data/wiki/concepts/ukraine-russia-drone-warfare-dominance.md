## Operational details

- Ukraine possesses world-leading ground and air drone technology capable of defeating peer adversaries such as Russia, leading the United States to request access to Ukrainian drone technology (Source 12: 'Ukraine leads the world in drone technology' and 'the Ukrainians are the ones with access to ground and air drone technology capable of beating and defeating peer adversaries i.e. russia')
- Ukraine has been conducting strikes against targets inside Moscow and around the city, demonstrating extended strike reach (Source 2: 'Ukraine is now comfortably striking the targets inside of Moscow and around the city')
- Ukraine has been systematically shutting down Russian oil and natural gas terminals to cripple Russia's economic capacity to fund the war (Source 14: 'Ukraine has been shutting down Russia's oil and natural gas terminals so that the Russian economy cannot generate the revenue it needs to continue functioning and to continue waging its war')
- Russia has deployed FPV drones and AI-enabled systems to target Ukrainian civilians, including UN vehicles and civilian vehicles, in what sources describe as a 'human safari' (Source 15: 'the Russian military has conducted what is referred to as a human safari that is to say all vehicles all people in HSN are targeted by drones' and 'Russians have proudly published videos of their FPV drones striking old women in wheelchairs')
- Europe is developing counter-drone capabilities including the Sky Ranger 30 mobile short-range air defense system with programmable cannons using airburst ammunition, now being adopted by six NATO armies (Source 18: 'Sky Ranger needs about 4 seconds of footage in the phrase airburst ammunition' and 'europe is trying to fix one of the most embarrassing gaps in modern Western armies the ability to protect moving troops from drones')
- Ukraine's drone deal with the United States remains stuck in bureaucratic limbo while deals with Europe and Gulf States proceed, with even the EU being described as more efficient for weapons procurement than the US in this case (Source 4: 'Ukraine is saying that its drone deal with the United States is stuck in bureaucratic limbo while its deals with Europe and Gulf States move ahead' and 'Getting the Europeans to purchase artillery shells for the Ukrainians took literally so long that the Ukrainians found how to fight without artillery')
- The United States Marine Corps is developing its own drone systems such as the Neuros Archer one-way attack drone with Kraken kinetic electronic arming and safe device, representing US efforts to catch up in drone warfare (Source 11: 'United States Marine Corps invited me to view a nearos Archer oneway attack drone armed with a Kraken kinetic electronic arming and safe device')
- Ukraine is now taking back more territory from Russia than Russia is gaining, with the stalemate having ended in Ukraine's favor according to analysis across multiple perspectives (Source 16: 'Ukraine is now taking back more land from Russia than Russia is taking from Ukraine on a consistent basis')
