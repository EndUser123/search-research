## Operational details

- Videos may use reaction commentary where a creator responds to or analyzes external content, as seen in Source 1 where a creator reacts to someone changing their political stance
- Interview formats involve direct questioning between participants, exemplified by Source 3's Piers Morgan questioning John Spencer on military ethics
- Debate or discussion formats present opposing viewpoints, with Source 2 showing a conversational exchange exploring religious greeting practices
- Educational or instructional dialogue presents information through conversational delivery, with Source 5 using a direct address style to explain legal responses
- The tone can shift between informal conversational styles and formal interview styles within the same video series
- Video length and editing approaches vary, with some using extended unedited dialogue and others employing concise segments
