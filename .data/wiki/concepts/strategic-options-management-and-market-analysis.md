## Operational details

- Diagonal spreads for high volatility stocks: Selling a higher-IV, front-dated call while buying a lower-strike, longer-dated call to exploit IV differentials and gain bullish directional exposure (Source 7, Source 20).
- Short puts for stock acquisition: Using out-of-the-money puts to acquire shares at an effective cost lower than the current market price or retaining premium if not assigned (Source 14, Source 20).
- 0DTE management protocols: Utilizing zero-days-to-expiration spreads on SPX with specific profit targets (25-50%) and strict exit rules to manage gamma and theta risk (Source 9, Source 13).
