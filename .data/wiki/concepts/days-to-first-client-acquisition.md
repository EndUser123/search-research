## Operational details

- Discovery calls emphasize gathering comprehensive information about the client's business and AI automation needs rather than extensive talking
- The LRP framework (Listen, Repeat, Poke) structures discovery calls to ensure alignment between provider and client expectations
- Cold outreach combined with automation can generate significant sales opportunities without requiring existing followers or content presence
- Personalization at scale using four-step automation processes enables rapid client outreach effectiveness
- Rapid monetization timelines have been documented, with some practitioners signing first clients within 5-7 days of starting outreach
- Hackathon participation represents an alternative acquisition path where completed AI projects can win prize money within 14-21 days
- No prior coding background is required, as demonstrated by non-developers winning automation competitions and building successful systems
- Building in public through content creation establishes trust before discovery calls, though it is not strictly necessary for client acquisition
