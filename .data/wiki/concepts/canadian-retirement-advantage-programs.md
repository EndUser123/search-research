## Operational details

- Retiring at age 55 can be financially advantageous over waiting until 65 due to the '3x Rule' approach, where running actual numbers shows unexpected outcomes favoring earlier retirement with a solid plan
- CPP and OAS benefits have no earnings limit in Canada, allowing retirees to work and collect government benefits simultaneously without withholding or clawback
- Property tax relief programs exist for Canadian homeowners aged 65 and older, including options to freeze, reduce, or eliminate property tax bills, though these require active application
