- The source notes that beta-hydroxybutyrate is another agonist for GPR109a, suggesting a connection between ketone metabolism and niacin signaling
- Excessive niacin intake produces a well-known flushing effect, mentioned as common knowledge
- Niacin is readily available in vitamin supplements
