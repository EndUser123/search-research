## Operational details

- LUCAS one-way attack drones integrate Shield AI's Hivemind autonomy software to enable coordinated swarming behavior
- Collaborative autonomy allows multiple drones to distribute targets, prioritize threats, and conduct evasive maneuvers collectively
- The system employs two-way data links enabling real-time coordination and adaptive mission execution
- Autonomy software enables execution of coordinated attacks without continuous human control or intervention
- Drone designs emphasize cost reduction to enable mass deployment while maintaining combat effectiveness
- Anduril's Roadrunner M interceptor features autonomous capabilities with operator-controlled abort options if targets prove non-hostile
- Roadrunner interceptor combines autonomous flight profiles with vertical landing capability for reuse after engagement
