## Operational details

- The Gordie Howe International Bridge project ($6.4 billion, fully funded by Canada) between Windsor and Detroit faced delay after Trump threatened to block its opening unless the US received some form of compensation
- Trump proposed 10% tariffs against Canada, with sources noting these tariffs were deemed illegal by the US Supreme Court
- Trump suggested making Canada the 51st state, which Canadian officials explicitly rejected
- Canada implemented boycotts of US products including alcohol and travel to the United States in response to American pressure
- Former NDP leader Tom Mulcair characterized PM Carney's approach as refusing to 'take the bait' from Trump
