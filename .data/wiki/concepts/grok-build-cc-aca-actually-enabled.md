## Summary

The cc-aca-* suite (Claude-Code-compat advisory/check/autonomy plugins) is **actually enabled and firing** in this Grok Build runtime. Verified by `grok inspect --json` (2026-07-20). The prior conversation transcripts repeatedly asserted this suite was disabled and the safety net was missing. That assertion was wrong.

## Key Findings

- **55 plugins enabled, 0 plugins disabled** at the plugin level (per `grok inspect --json`).
- **62 hooks loaded** in total, distributed across events:
  - `PreToolUse`: 12 — skill-guard router, cc-aca-safety, cc-aca-investigation, cc-aca-authority, cc-aca-observability, cc-aca-reasoning, cc-aca-epistemic, cc-aca-session, cc-aca-sdlc, cc-skills-utils, model-router, prompt-enhancer
  - `PostToolUse`: 15 — cc-aca-epistemic, cc-aca-observability, cc-aca-reasoning, plus 12 more (mostly cc-aca-* routers and cc-skills-utils)
  - `Stop`: 1 (cc-skills-utils router)
  - `SubagentStop`: 1 (`SubagentStop_cjk_drift_detector.py`)
  - `SessionStart`: 1 (the M1 `active_surface_snapshot.py`)
  - `Notification`: 2 (`Notification_voice_hook.py`, `log_hook.py`)
- **Compat layer is narrow.** Per `externalCompat.cells` in inspect: `compat.claude.hooks = false` (explicit), `compat.cursor.hooks = false` (explicit). So Claude-side and Cursor-side `hooks.json` files are **not** being scanned. The 12 PreToolUse / 15 PostToolUse hooks come from plugin-bundled hooks + Grok-native `~/.grok/hooks/*.json`, **not** from `~/.claude/settings.json`.
- **339 permission rules loaded** from `P:/.claude/settings.local.json` via `compat.claude.rules = true`, but **at least 16 are silently dropped** because of unrecognized tool prefixes: `SlashCommand(*)`, `Skill(*)`, and a stack of `PowerShell(...)` rules. The recognized prefixes per `22-permissions-and-safety.md:287-288` are only `Bash`, `Read`, `Edit`, `Grep`, `MCPTool`, `WebFetch`, `WebSearch` (plus lowercase variants). The dropped rules are expected behavior, not a bug.
- **The actual enforcement story.** The cc-aca-* PreToolUse gates fire on every tool call. They are the safety net that the original conversation transcript assumed was missing. If a plan-mode approval led to implementation that touched a disabled entity, the PreToolUse gate would catch it (subject to the entity being in the gate's deny list — which the M1 active-surface snapshot doesn't surface, see related page).
