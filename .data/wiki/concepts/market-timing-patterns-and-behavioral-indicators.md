## Operational details

- Extended sideways consolidation periods lasting months to years frequently precede significant price explosions in commodities like gold, silver, and related ETFs (GDX, GDXJ)
- Historical IPO timing correlates with market peaks; major offerings from Goldman Sachs Trading Corporation (1928), Intel (1970s), AT&T (2000), and SpaceX (projected) occurred near market extremes
- High-frequency trading outcomes require large sample sizes for statistical validity; testing 100,000 strategies across 1,000 stocks yielded 2.3 million backtests with only 2,226 passing all filters
- CBOE 1-month correlation indicator readings below 8 historically signal impending equity market drawdowns by measuring single-stock implied volatility relative to index volatility
- Excessive call buying, reflected in rich single-stock call pricing, creates self-reinforcing correction potential when positions become too expensive
- S&P 500 momentum analysis over 54 years shows normal market returns cluster in the 1-7% six-week range, with extreme moves beyond ±28% representing statistically rare tail events
