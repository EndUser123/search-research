# Drawstring Knot Technique

## Decision context

**Definition:** A method for securing drawstrings on athletic wear using a specific knotting approach that maintains tension during physical activity while allowing adjustable loosening and tightening. The technique involves forming an ampersand-shaped loop with one drawstring end, threading it through and around the other end, and creating a secondary loop for tension adjustment.

## Operational details

- One drawstring end is shaped into an ampersand-like form to serve as the primary loop for knotting
- The loop is threaded over the standing end and around the bottom before pulling tight to secure the knot
- A thumb loop is created in the loose end that allows gathering of excess material for adding tension
- The same thumb loop can be used to release tension by pulling the gathered loose end
- The knot resists loosening during movement and activity
- Tension can be adjusted by manipulating the thumb loop and loose end without fully untying the knot
- The technique can be demonstrated from a first-person perspective showing the sew-in point at the back of the garment
