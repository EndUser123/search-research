# First Principles Thinking

## Decision context

**Definition:** First principles thinking is a reasoning approach that deconstructs problems into their most fundamental, verifiable components and reconstructs understanding from those basic truths, rather than relying on analogical reasoning or inherited assumptions.

## Operational details

- The approach involves decomposing a problem into its most basic, self-evident elements that can be verified as true
- Each underlying assumption is challenged and tested rather than accepted by default
- Solutions are rebuilt from verified foundational elements, enabling reframing of problems without inherited constraints
- The technique traces to Aristotle's philosophical concept of first principles
- The approach appears in mental model frameworks for improving decision-making and problem-solving
- The method contrasts with reasoning by analogy, which relies on borrowed assumptions from similar past situations
