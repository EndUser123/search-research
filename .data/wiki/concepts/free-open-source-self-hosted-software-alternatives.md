- Many open-source projects have achieved significant community adoption measured by GitHub stars and active development, with projects like Odysseus reaching tens of thousands of stars rapidly [Source 2: 'Odysius... rocketed to something like 78,000 stars'] [Source 17: 'OpenClaw is a free AI assistant you host yourself']
