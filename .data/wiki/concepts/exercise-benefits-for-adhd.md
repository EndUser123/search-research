## Operational details

- Exercise boosts neurotransmitters that are involved in stimulant medication, potentially supporting focus and emotional regulation in ADHD brains
- Regular exercise may contribute to brain growth in individuals with ADHD
- Personal experimentation with different exercise types is needed to find what works for each individual's ADHD brain
- The friction of starting exercise (making micro-decisions) can be a barrier for ADHD brains, similar to productivity app challenges
- Urgency and immediate feedback appear to help with engagement for ADHD individuals
