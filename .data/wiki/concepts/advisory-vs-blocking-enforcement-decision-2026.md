# Advisory vs Blocking: Measurement-First Enforcement Strategy

**Host provenance:** grok
**Date:** 2026-07-29
**Status:** DECISION — load-bearing architectural choice

## Decision

PGM (proposal-grounding-monitor) remains advisory-only (v0.2.0). Blocking mode (Phase 4) is deferred until four criteria are met: ≥50 detections, a labeling protocol exists, Wilson 95% CI upper bound ≤30% FP on labeled subset, and ≥1 natural-use REVISED_AFTER_INSPECTION outcome. Operator sign-off required.

## Context

The `~/.grok/AGENTS.md` rule "search before proposing" was originally framed as a target for mechanical enforcement via a hook. A `/design` run (d8173a98, 2026-07-29) discovered:

1. PGM already exists and is enabled — but was silently dead due to a payload field bug
2. The wiki's enforcement hierarchy (`enforcement-hierarchy-and-compaction-strategy.md:158-163`) classifies "Did I search before proposing?" as a prompt-level concern: "can't mechanically verify intent"
3. The exec-gate plugin was disabled 2026-07-20 for "false positives and over-aggressive friction" — the same disablement risk applies to any blocking enforcement

## Rationale

- **Advisory restoration first:** PGM's payload bug means zero empirical data exists on what the rule catches today. Measurement is the prerequisite for any enforcement decision.
- **Blocking without measurement is disablement risk:** exec-gate precedent shows that broad blocking with high FP causes operator disablement — the worst outcome because it eliminates even advisory enforcement.
- **The wiki is right about intent verification:** regex cannot distinguish "proposing a solution" from "paraphrasing the user" or "writing a test assertion." A two-layer regex→LLM-judge approach could help, but needs FP-rate data first.
- **Scope extension is experiment, not mandate completion:** adding qualifying categories LOWERS the repair trigger rate (more reads count as grounding). Must measure post-fix baseline before changing scope.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Ship blocking now:** high disablement risk (exec-gate precedent). Rejected.
- **New sibling hook:** duplicates PGM's 117-test scaffold. Rejected.
- **Remove PGM:** loses measurement infrastructure. Rejected.
- **Prompt-only:** ~50% compliance ceiling; degrades under closure pressure. Rejected.

## Falsifier

If Phase 3 measurement shows PGM's regex prefilter has >50% FP rate on labeled data, the entire hook-based approach is suspect and Phase 4 should re-evaluate whether prompt-level is the right ceiling.

## Shelf life

Revisit after Day 58 of the phased rollout (approximately 2026-09-25). If labeling protocol (OQ-9) is not resolved by then, the measurement-first strategy has stalled and the operator should decide whether to (a) invest in labeling, (b) accept advisory-only as the permanent ceiling, or (c) remove PGM.
