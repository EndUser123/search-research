## Operational details

- Creatine monohydrate is one of the most studied compounds with over 700 human trials, showing effects on muscle building, brain protection, and fat cell shrinkage, though uptake issues after age 40 may reduce effectiveness for many users
- Glycine deficiency creates a potential daily deficit of approximately 10 grams, contributing to chronic inflammation, poor sleep, metabolic dysfunction, and cardiovascular issues
- Mitochondrial electron transport chain dysfunction can manifest as exercise intolerance during transitions between rest and activity, affecting the body's ability to ramp up energy production to match sudden demand
- Insulin resistance drives metabolic dysfunction and cognitive decline, with glucose tolerance testing identified as an insightful diagnostic tool for identifying impaired carbohydrate metabolism
- Body composition targets around 15% body fat for men over 35 support natural testosterone recovery, improved sleep quality, and sustained energy levels, while exceeding 20% body fat triggers hormonal shifts converting testosterone to estrogen
- Resistance training addresses leg strength decline after 50 that walking alone cannot prevent, since muscles require resistance stimulus to maintain mass and strength
- Peptide compounds affecting energy levels, glucose homeostasis, insulin sensitivity, and fatty acid metabolism have gained regulatory attention, with WADA adding certain peptides to prohibited lists and compounding restrictions enacted in the US
- Clinical trial dosing schedules are designed to find maximum tolerated doses rather than optimal individual doses, often requiring lower maintenance doses than trial endpoints suggest
- Appetite control, fat mobilization, and metabolic enhancement represent three interconnected physiological systems that must work together for effective body composition changes
