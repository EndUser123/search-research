## Operational details

- GLP-1 receptor agonists are identified as a pharmaceutical approach for weight management, though muscle loss may account for up to 40% of total weight loss when relying solely on scale measurements
- Magnesium deficiency affects an estimated 45% of the US population and is directly linked to elevated cortisol, insulin resistance, and chronic inflammation
- Magnesium absorption through oral supplementation averages approximately 10%, though a specific colonic pathway may increase absorption to 30-40% in some individuals
- Cranberry consumption was studied in a human clinical trial of 110 participants published in BMC Complementary Medicine and Therapeutics for its effects on visceral fat and hepatic fat accumulation
- Intermittent fasting outcomes vary significantly based on age, with meta-analysis of 28 randomized controlled trials (nearly 1,800 participants) showing different response patterns across age groups (under 30, 30-45, and 45+)
- Matriptase (MOD) was added to the World Anti-Doping Agency 2025 prohibited list, indicating effectiveness in human subjects for energy, glucose homeostasis, insulin sensitivity, and fatty acid metabolism
- Compounded drugs containing MOD were restricted on September 29, 2023 under Section 503A of the Federal Food, Drug and Cosmetic Act due to immunogenicity concerns
- A U-shaped dose response curve for selenium means both deficiency and excess pose health risks, with Brazil nuts containing highly variable selenium levels ranging from 1 to over 500 micrograms per nut
- Retatrutide is described as an unapproved drug still in clinical trials being sold illegally online, with concerns about counterfeit versions
- Fish oil supplementation was associated with faster cognitive decline in a study using the ADNI database, though limitations in the data warrant careful interpretation
