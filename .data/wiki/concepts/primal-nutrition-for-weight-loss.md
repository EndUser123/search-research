## Operational details

- The diet emphasizes real, whole foods: meat, fish, eggs, vegetables, fruit, and tubers, avoiding processed items and added sugars
- The approach is distinct from keto, low-carb, and carnivore diets, focusing instead on food quality rather than macronutrient restriction
- Simple eating protocols with repeated daily meals (e.g., beef, bacon, eggs, butter) can yield rapid weight loss results when consistently maintained
- A structured program using this nutritional framework has been documented to help over 1,000 participants lose weight, build muscle, reduce medications, and reverse chronic conditions
- One source reported losing 50 pounds in 62 days using simplified food selection without calorie counting or overthinking
