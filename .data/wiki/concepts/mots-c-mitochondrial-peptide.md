# MOTS-C Mitochondrial Peptide

## Decision context

**Definition:** MOTS-C is a 16 amino acid peptide encoded in mitochondrial DNA (distinct from nuclear DNA) that functions as a regulator of cellular energy metabolism. Endogenous levels increase with exercise and decline approximately 21% with aging, with higher levels associated with improved metabolic function and insulin sensitivity.

## Operational details

- Encoded by mitochondrial DNA rather than nuclear DNA, making it structurally distinct from most other peptides
- Administered in animal models, MOTS-C has demonstrated improvements in insulin sensitivity, endurance, and resistance to metabolic dysfunction
- Operates through AMPK activation, described as the body's fuel gauge—when activated, cells exhibit increased fat oxidation, improved glucose uptake, enhanced insulin sensitivity, and increased mitochondrial biogenesis
- Using MOTS-C in isolation may yield suboptimal results compared to combined protocols addressing overall mitochondrial health
- In mice studies, MOTS-C administration has been associated with extended lifespan; in diabetic rat models, it reversed cardiac hypertrophy
- Retatrutide combined with MOTS-C represents a dual approach targeting both appetite/metabolic signaling and cellular energy utilization
