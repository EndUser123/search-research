## Operational details

- Retatrutide is an investigational drug formally under study, not approved for clinical use, yet available through illegal online sales channels, with influencers promoting counterfeit versions despite unknown safety profiles
- The Verve 102 trial represents a gene editing approach targeting cholesterol-containing lipoproteins to potentially eliminate atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease by reducing plaque retention in artery walls
- Health Canada recently approved the first injection for sleep apnea, representing a pharmacologic alternative to CPAP devices for managing this condition linked to heart disease and diabetes
- A workplace-based weight loss study reported that 100% of participants achieved weight reduction and employers observed significant reductions in medication expenditure
- Systemic distribution of lipid nanoparticles has been documented in multiple tissues including ovaries, testes, brain, heart, kidneys, and liver, with detection of vaccine-derived spike protein associated with immune cell inflammation in cardiac and hepatic tissue
- Clinical trials for antiviral therapeutics including MBP134 and remdesivir are being conducted to evaluate mortality reduction in patients with Bundibugyo virus disease
