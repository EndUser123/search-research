## Operational details

- MOTS-c is a 16 amino acid mitochondrial-derived peptide whose levels drop approximately 21% with aging in humans, with sources indicating it may influence energy levels, glucose homeostasis, insulin sensitivity, and fatty acid metabolism
- Zone 2 training at low intensity is proposed to optimize fat oxidation, with sources describing a protocol of 180 minutes weekly over 6 weeks to improve fat-burning capacity
- Curcumin has been documented to interfere with multiple cancer cell metabolic pathways including glucose uptake, glycolysis, lactate production, and HIF1-alpha signaling
- Carbohydrate intake during fat loss phases is described as supporting insulin signaling which promotes leptin secretion, T3 conversion at the liver, and maintenance of sex hormone levels
- Cancer cells consume glucose at rates described as 10 to 30-fold higher than normal cells, enabling PET scan visualization due to high sugar absorption rates
- Meal consistency strategies involving repeated consumption of the same meals are described as reducing daily food decisions (approximately 200 daily) and supporting adherence
- Exercise snacks of approximately one minute duration performed hourly are described as a method to break up sedentary time and reduce independent disease risk factors associated with sitting six hours or more
