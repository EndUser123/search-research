# Sequential Falsification for Hypothesis Validation

## Decision context

**Definition:** Sequential falsification is a scientific methodology adapted for AI agents wherein hypotheses are tested through systematic attempts to disprove them, rather than seeking confirmatory evidence. The approach decomposes complex hypotheses into testable sub-hypotheses and validates them through iterative agent-driven falsification attempts.

## Operational details

- The method employs agentic sequential falsifications to break down complex hypotheses into simpler, testable sub-hypotheses
- Each sub-hypothesis undergoes structured validation designed to identify potential falsifications
- The approach aims to validate hypotheses rather than merely seeking confirmation of expected outcomes
- Falsification follows a philosophical tradition originating from Popper's falsificationism, requiring that a hypothesis be stated in a way that permits clear disconfirmation through counterexamples
