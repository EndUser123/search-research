# Smart Glasses Display Technologies and Ecosystem

## Decision context

**Definition:** Smart glasses are wearable devices with integrated display systems that overlay digital information onto the user's field of view. These devices range from basic notification mirrors to advanced augmented reality systems, with current products differentiating through display technology (waveguide vs. micro OLED), weight, battery life, and AI integration capabilities.

## Operational details

- Even Realities G2 utilize monochrome waveguide displays at 640x350 resolution per eye, offering glancible text and notifications through integrated displays in both eyes
- MemoMind glasses feature dual display monochrome setups with multiple frame styles (round and rectangular) and color options for customization
- XREAL One incorporates a 68-inch micro OLED display from Sony with a 50-degree field of view, representing a projection-style screen that follows the user
- The MemoMind One prioritizes a no-camera design philosophy, incorporating stereo heads-up display and full audio while maintaining a weight under 50 grams
- MIRA glasses introduce AI-powered memory capabilities that passively listen, remember conversations, and retrieve stored information on demand
- Price points span from $50 budget camera-only glasses to $950+ ecosystems including prescription lenses and controller accessories
- Audio integration varies significantly; MemoMind One addresses prior complaints about Even Realities G2 lacking audio by adding arm and audio features
- XREAL XBAP a01+ AR glasses launched at $299 as the world's lightest premium AR glasses at that price point
