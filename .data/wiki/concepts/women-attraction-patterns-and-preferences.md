## Operational details

- Younger women are described as being attracted to older men because older men possess maturity that younger men have not yet developed, providing a sense of provision and stability
- Women are characterized as being aware that older men may harbor preconceived notions about being perceived as creepy, yet the sources suggest women are generally open to dating older men
- Attraction to older men is linked to women being introduced to mature energy at a young age, which shapes their preferences as they age
- Women may be drawn to financially successful and high-performing men, though this can lead to losing aspects of their own personal identity
- The concept of chivalry remains important to some women, with expectations around traditional gestures like opening doors
- A scientific study of newly married couples found the mean age of male participants was 25.46 years and female participants was 24.78 years, showing average age gaps in early marriages
- The sources indicate some disagreement about whether women prioritize kindness, with one source arguing science proves women want something other than kindness
