# Consistency Through Focused Trading Setup

## Decision context

**Definition:** Achieving consistent trading profitability involves narrowing one's approach to a single, well-understood setup rather than trading multiple strategies. This method builds confidence through repetition and predictable outcomes over time.

## Operational details

- Trading multiple setups simultaneously (breakouts, reversals, pullbacks, retests) leads to inconsistent results and account volatility.
- Focusing on one 'boring' setup with a high winning percentage creates consistency in results.
- Consistency in trading outcomes generates confidence, which enables scaling position size from one contract to multiple contracts.
- In competitive trading environments, being first to act on an opportunity provides control over the trade.
- The first hour of market operation exhibits predictable behavior patterns that can be exploited with a disciplined setup.
