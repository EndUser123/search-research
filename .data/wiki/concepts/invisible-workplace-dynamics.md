## Operational details

- Senior promotion decisions are made in calibration meetings where formal qualifications are necessary but rarely sufficient for advancement [Source 4]
- The most talented person in a room with the best numbers, strongest team, and cleanest delivery wins promotion only one in five times, sometimes one in ten [Source 4]
- A specific phrase used in calibration meetings can permanently end careers without the affected individual ever learning what happened [Source 4]
- Status perception occurs through two channels: visual image (appearance, posture) and vocal image (how one uses their voice) [Source 9]
- Voice-based status judgments form beliefs about credibility, conviction, and whether someone should be taken seriously [Source 9]
- Certain personality types report feeling disconnected from major cultural phenomena like sports and celebrity culture, observing but not absorbing [Source 7]
- Social psychology research documents 'basking in reflected glory' where individuals align more with winning teams after victories [Source 7]
