## Operational details

- The US cattle herd has contracted to its lowest level in 75 years, creating persistent supply tightness that supports elevated beef prices
- A New World Screwworm outbreak detected in Texas in 2024 represents the first occurrence in the US in 60 years, prompting a state of emergency declaration and USDA ground response as a potential agricultural emergency
- JBS USA, the world's largest meat packer (Brazilian-owned), announced closure of its Soderton, Pennsylvania facility in June 2024, eliminating processing capacity for over 2,000 head of cattle daily
- Ground beef reached $6.89 per pound, the highest recorded price since the government began tracking in 1984
- Trade policy uncertainty with Canada as a net beef exporter creates additional market volatility concerns
- Bill Gates has become the single largest private US farmland owner with 270,000 acres across 18 states, often acquired through shell companies, while simultaneously investing tens of millions in synthetic meat companies including Beyond Meat, Impossible Foods, and Memphis Meats
- Jeff Bezos committed $100 million through his Bezos Earth Fund to establish research centers for plant-based, fermented, and lab-grown meat alternatives
- Gates stated in a 2021 MIT Technology Review interview that wealthy nations should move to 100% synthetic beef and suggested using regulation to shift consumer demand away from conventional beef
