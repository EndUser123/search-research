- Bystanders intervened during the attack, including one individual who wielded a hurling stick to help stop the assailant
- The stabbing triggered nights of violent unrest across Belfast, with masked men setting multiple fires and targeting a house reportedly used by immigrants
- A total of 27 people were made homeless as a result of the riots, with children reportedly dragged from burning homes
- Police stated there was no indication the attack was terrorism-related, though the accused had arrived in Northern Ireland 3 years ago and claimed asylum

- Public figures have used such incidents to argue for addressing immigration concerns, while critics frame such arguments as exploiting tragedies
- The incident occurred amid ongoing debates about two-tier policing in the UK, with some advocates arguing certain communities receive preferential treatment
- Counter-arguments emphasize that individual criminal acts should not be generalized to entire communities or religions
