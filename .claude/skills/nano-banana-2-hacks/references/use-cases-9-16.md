# Nano Banana 2 Use Cases 9-16

Detailed prompt templates and techniques for use cases 9 through 16.

## 9. Age Progression & Regression
**Use Case**: Character design, historical recreation, speculative casting, VFX

### Age Progression
```
Upload: Photo of person (20s-30s)
Prompt: "Age him to 65 years old"
```

**Results**:
- Natural wrinkles around eyes and forehead
- Gray hair with realistic texture
- Skin tone changes
- Subtle jowl development
- Biological aging markers

### Age Regression
```
Upload: Photo of older person
Prompt: "Make him look 18"
```

**Quality**:
- Smooth skin, youthful features
- Brighter eyes
- Realistic de-aging
- No uncanny valley
- No plastic smoothing

**Production Grade**: VFX quality without After Effects or dedicated software

## 11. Infographic Creation
**Use Case**: Education, marketing, complex information visualization

```
Prompt: "Create a professional infographic explaining [topic] with labeled diagrams and step-by-step illustrations"
```

**Examples**:
- Photosynthesis: Arrows showing flow, labeled chloroplasts/stomata, color-coded stages
- Smartphone evolution: Timeline layout, device illustrations, specs under each milestone

**Output Quality**:
- Clean educational graphics
- Readable explanatory text
- Visual hierarchy guidance
- Professional design standards

## 12. Multi-Object Merging
**Use Case**: Interior design, scene composition, product visualization

```
Upload: Grid of 20 individual items (furniture, objects)
Prompt: "Merge all 20 furniture items into one cohesive modern living room interior"
```

**Composition Handling**:
- Natural item integration (sofa against back wall)
- Proper height relationships (coffee table in front)
- Logical positioning (lamps providing light)
- Proper scale relationships
- Realistic shadows throughout
- Consistent lighting across scene

**Application**: Generate individual pieces, let AI compose into realistic 3D space without modeling

## 15. Annotation-Guided Editing
**Use Case**: Precise edits, directed modifications, surgical control

**Workflow**:
1. Open image in basic image editor (Microsoft Paint)
2. Draw crude red arrows pointing to areas
3. Add text notes with default font
4. Upload marked-up image
5. Prompt: "Follow all annotations and instructions, then remove the annotation marks"

**Benefits**:
- Sketch directly on image to guide AI
- Exact spatial instructions
- Clean final result
- Surgical level control

## 16. Historical Photo Restoration
**Use Case**: Family archives, historical preservation, nostalgia projects

```
Upload: Faded black and white photo (1940s, creases, spots, deteriorated)
Prompt: "Restore and colorize this photo with accurate period-appropriate colors"
```

**Historical Accuracy**:
- Muted tones matching era's film stock
- Period-appropriate clothing dye technology
- Proper skin rendering
- Contextual clothing colors
- Military uniforms in correct olive drab
- Era-specific dress colors

**Quality**: Understands historical context, doesn't randomly colorize
