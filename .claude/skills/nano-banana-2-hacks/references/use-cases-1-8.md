# Nano Banana 2 Use Cases 1-8

Detailed prompt templates and techniques for use cases 1 through 8.

---

## 1. Seasonal Transformations
**Use Case**: Real estate, marketing, location visualization

**Technique**:
```
Upload a location photo, then prompt:
"Show this same location across all four seasons in a grid"
```

**Results**:
- Spring: Blooming flowers, fresh vegetation
- Summer: Full green trees, vibrant life
- Autumn: Orange/red leaves, scattered foliage
- Winter: Snow coverage, atmospheric changes

**Key**: Maintains identical camera angle, composition, and building structure

---

## 2. Product Photography & Mockups
**Use Case**: Brand design, marketing materials, product visualization

### Logo Integration
```
Upload: Stock photo of person holding product + Your logo design
Prompt: "Realistically replace the logo on the cup"
```

**What it handles**:
- Wraps design around surface curvature
- Adds finger shadows and grip effects
- Adjusts gloss and reflections to match lighting
- Natural material integration

### Material Transformation
```
Upload: Simple geometric logo
Prompt: "Retexture this logo into melted chocolate on white marble, studio lighting"
```

**Results**:
- Realistic material rendering (chocolate, metal, glass)
- Proper reflections on marble surfaces
- Natural pooling and drips
- Professional studio lighting

## 3. Architectural Cross-Sections
**Use Case**: Architecture, design visualization, educational content

```
Upload: Exterior building photo
Prompt: "Create a detailed architectural cross-section showing all interior rooms and floors"
```

**Output Quality**:
- Professional cutaway views
- Every room visible
- Floor levels clearly defined
- Staircase connections
- Furniture placement that makes spatial sense

**Note**: Not for construction drawings, perfect for presentations and early concept visualization

---

## 4. Object Removal & Cleanup
**Use Case**: Photo editing, portrait retouching, scene cleanup

```
Upload: Photo with unwanted elements
Prompt: "Remove [specific object] completely"
```

**Capabilities**:
- Facial hair removal with natural skin texture
- Jewelry, tattoos, logos elimination
- Background person removal
- Object cleanup from tables

**Quality**: Zero artifacts, zero smudging, proper lighting and shadow reconstruction

---

## 5. Lighting Redesign
**Use Case**: Cinematography, photography education, lighting recreation

### Reverse Engineering Lighting Setups
```
Upload: Cinematic scene or photo
Prompt: "Show a professional lighting diagram with all equipment positions and specifications"
```

**Output Details**:
- Key light position (45 with softbox modifier)
- Fill light placement (camera left)
- Hair/Back light positioning
- Color temperature notes (3200K tungsten, 5600K daylight)
- Complete gaffer diagram

**Application**: Learn cinematography, recreate film looks, understand lighting setups

---

## 6. Comic Book Panels With Dialogue
**Use Case**: Storytelling, graphic novels, narrative visualization

### Single-Page Creation
```
Upload: Detailed panel description with:
- Framing for each panel (medium shot, close-up, wide)
- Exact dialogue for speech bubbles
- Character positions and action beats
```

**Output**: Crisp speech bubbles with legible text, proper panel layout

### Long-Form Storytelling Hack
```
Upload: Finished comic page
Prompt: "Continue this fight scene for three more panels"
```

**Results**:
- Analyzes existing story continuity
- Matches art style exactly
- Generates logical next sequence
- Maintains character positions and action flow

---

## 7. Technical Exploded Views
**Use Case**: Product design, engineering, patent documentation, marketing materials

```
Upload: Product photo (headphones, device, etc.)
Prompt: "Create an accurate exploded view diagram with all internal components labeled, professional white background"
```

**Output Includes**:
- Separated components (drivers, batteries, circuits)
- Realistic component placement
- Professional patent-filing quality
- Educational diagram standard

**Note**: Infers internals from product knowledge, not X-ray scanning

---

## 8. Style Transfer With References
**Use Case**: Creative reimagining, brand consistency, artistic transformation

```
Upload two images:
1. Reference image with desired style (e.g., halftone dots, bold outlines)
2. Target photo to transform

Prompt: "Transform the street photo into the exact style of the animated reference image"
```

**Style Elements Cloned**:
- Half-tone dot shading
- Bold graphic outlines
- Comic book color separation
- Film graininess
- Texture replication

**Key**: Studies and replicates exact style, not just approximation
