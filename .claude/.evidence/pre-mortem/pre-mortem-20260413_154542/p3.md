1.2. 1.5. [HIGH] (adversarial-compliance) — `gap_resolution_tracker.py:419`: Verification records store raw gap IDs (`gap_ids=[gap_id]`) but lookups use `_normalize_gap_key()`. Gap IDs with ephemeral suffixes won't match across storage/lookup boundary, causing repeated re-verification.
