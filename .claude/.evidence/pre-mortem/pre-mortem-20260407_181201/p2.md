## Severity Calibration Issues

### 1. ReDoS Severity: MEDIUM vs "Proper Protection"

**Issue**: adversarial-security says MEDIUM, adversarial-logic says "proper protection"

**Evidence**:
- adversarial-security SEC-001: "MEDIUM severity ReDoS risk" with specific attack scenario ("alternating word boundary characters within 100-char window")
- adversarial-logic summary: "proper ReDoS protection (.{0,100}? limit)" — no severity assigned, presented as already handled
