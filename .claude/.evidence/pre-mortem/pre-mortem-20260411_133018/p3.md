## Logical Gaps & Inconsistencies

1.1. [HIGH] VERIFICATION_LANGUAGE_PATTERNS `^i` line-start anchor at claim_patterns.py:251 causes mid-paragraph verification statements to not cancel behavioral assertions, producing false positive blocks. The `^i` pattern only matches when "i" is at the START of a line. Verification statements mid-paragraph like "The system always blocks. I verified it" will NOT be detected as verification language. Fix: change `^i` to `\bi` for word-boundary match (adversarial-testing, claim_patterns.py:251)
