## Logical Gaps & Inconsistencies

### 1.1 [HIGH] T1→T2 Cascade Mischaracterizes Portalocker Behavior (Source: adversarial-logic, adversarial-critic)

**Location**: Step 2.5, lines 81-86

**Finding**: The cascade trace assumes lock timeout fires mid-operation and "cleanup runs without releasing lock properly." But portalocker raises `LockFailed` on timeout — it does not silently continue. The cascade conflates two distinct failure modes: T1 (lock timeout detection symptom) and T2 (write race cause). They are not causally linked.

**Evidence**: Step 2.5 line 82: "Lock timeout fires during session end" → line 83: "Cleanup runs without releasing lock properly" — this is physically incorrect for portalocker.

**Impact**: Cascade analysis overstates risk coupling. 3-step cascade is structurally flawed.
